# MyZubster Community Code of Conduct

MyZubster is an open-source project. Participation is open to people from different countries, backgrounds, professions, skill levels and public identities, provided they follow the project rules and applicable law.

## Our standard

We expect contributors to:

- treat other participants with respect;
- discuss ideas, code and evidence rather than attacking people;
- welcome questions from beginners and first-time contributors;
- give constructive, specific and actionable feedback;
- respect privacy and a contributor's choice to use a public alias;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes when better evidence appears;
- respect copyright, licensing, consent and repository boundaries;
- keep fictional/AI-generated material distinguishable from factual evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in MyZubster community spaces:

- harassment, discrimination, intimidation or threats;
- hate speech or targeted abuse;
- doxxing or publishing unnecessary private information;
- pressure to reveal a legal identity, private address, credentials, wallet seed or other sensitive data;
- impersonation of another contributor, organization or partner;
- knowingly fabricated evidence, fake affiliations, fake payments or misleading production claims;
- unauthorized security testing, credential theft, access-control bypass or attacks on third-party systems;
- publishing sensitive/restricted location or security information without authorization.

## Evidence and disagreement

Disagreement is allowed and useful. When facts are disputed, prefer public sources, reproducible tests, repository history and clearly labelled uncertainty.

An issue, PR, screenshot, AI image, ledger entry or public statement does not automatically prove deployment, payment, adoption, partnership or identity.

## Participation and identity

A legal name, KYC, cryptocurrency ownership or prior contribution is not required for ordinary participation. Contributors may use a public alias.

Participation does not imply employment, partnership, payment, endorsement, project ownership or verified identity.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject or remove contributions and interactions that violate these rules, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community, project security, privacy or legal compliance.

When possible, moderation decisions should be explained with reference to the relevant behavior or project rule.

## Scope

This code applies to official MyZubster repositories, issue and PR discussions, project-managed community spaces and other interactions where someone is representing MyZubster in an official project capacity.

**Build openly. Protect privacy. Separate evidence from claims. Treat contributors with respect.**
