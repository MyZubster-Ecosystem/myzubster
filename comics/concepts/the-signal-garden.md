# The Signal Garden

Submission for `COMIC-BNT-0001` / issue #569.

## Logline

When a neglected community planter is falsely blamed on its volunteer caretakers, a young cyclist uses MyZubster to turn a single observation into a transparent, reviewable trail that helps the neighborhood find the real problem and organize a repair.

## Premise

Mara, a fictional teenage bicycle courier, notices that the plants in a small public garden are wilting even though a handwritten sign says they were watered that morning. Rather than post an accusation, she records a careful observation through the MyZubster contribution workflow. The record attracts useful questions, a second observation, and a review that reveals a cracked irrigation connector hidden below the soil.

The story treats MyZubster as an open collaboration process: observe, sanitize, structure, review, improve, and publish. It does not portray an experimental component, reward, or proposed feature as guaranteed or production-ready.

## Audience and format

- Primary audience: readers aged 12 and older who enjoy hopeful near-future community stories.
- Secondary audience: new contributors who need an intuitive example of evidence-first collaboration.
- Suggested length: 6 comic pages, 3-5 panels per page.
- Tone: curious and energetic, with a grounded civic-technology focus rather than a superhero conflict.

## Main characters

- **Mara:** an observant bicycle courier who acts quickly but learns to separate a suspicion from verified evidence.
- **Ivo:** an older volunteer gardener who initially thinks the online report is an accusation.
- **Pip:** a fictional child from the neighborhood who supplies the decisive second observation: water pooling beside the planter after the timer runs.

All characters and the setting are original to this concept. No third-party franchise, protected character, or unauthorised likeness is used.

## Main conflict

The garden is dying, and an unverified social post has already accused its volunteers of neglect. Mara must resist the pressure to repeat a dramatic claim. She instead builds a reviewable MyZubster record while earning Ivo's trust and finding evidence strong enough to distinguish human neglect from an infrastructure fault.

## Eight-beat story outline

1. **A bad signal:** Mara brakes beside a bright mural and a row of wilted plants. The watering sign and the dry soil contradict each other. A phone notification shows a neighborhood post blaming the volunteers.
2. **Observation before accusation:** Mara photographs only the planter and public sign, excludes nearby faces and addresses, and drafts a MyZubster observation marked "cause unknown." The interface visually separates what she saw from what she suspects.
3. **The defensive gardener:** Ivo arrives and assumes Mara is collecting material to shame the volunteers. She shows him the neutral wording and invites him to add context instead of arguing in the comment thread.
4. **A structured trail:** Together they add the watering schedule, the visible soil condition, and a request for a second observation after the next timer cycle. The contribution becomes a small public checklist rather than a viral verdict.
5. **The missing piece:** Pip points out that a puddle appears behind the planter whenever the timer clicks. Mara and Ivo inspect the public-facing area without digging or entering restricted property and document the new clue.
6. **Review changes the story:** A community reviewer compares both observations and marks the original neglect claim unsupported. The evidence now points to a leaking connector, but the record still labels the diagnosis as awaiting physical confirmation.
7. **Repair and verification:** An authorised maintenance worker replaces the cracked connector. Mara records a follow-up after the next watering cycle: damp soil, no puddle, and recovering plants. The reviewer links the before-and-after evidence.
8. **A garden answers back:** Days later, Ivo pins a small QR code beside the planter so neighbors can inspect the public record and contribute future observations. Mara rides away as Pip waters a new seedling. The closing caption reads: "A useful signal does not shout. It lets others check it."

## MyZubster integration

The plot cannot resolve without the MyZubster workflow. The contribution preserves the distinction between observation and interpretation, gathers a second perspective, exposes the evidence to review, and links a follow-up result. This is a fictional story built around the project's documented goals of organizing public or authorized real-world observations into structured, reviewable information.
