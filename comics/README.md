# MyZubster Comics Bounty Program

The **MyZubster Comics Bounty Program** rewards verified creative work for the MyZubster universe. Rewards in this program are denominated **exclusively in MYZ**.

The program covers comic-related contributions such as:

- story concepts and scripts;
- character sheets and visual development;
- storyboards and page layouts;
- lettering and dialogue adaptation;
- finished comic pages;
- covers and promotional art;
- complete short comics or episodes.

## How it works

1. Choose an open `COMIC-BNT-*` GitHub issue.
2. Read the exact brief, deliverables, acceptance criteria and MYZ reward.
3. Comment on the issue before starting if the challenge requires claiming or coordination.
4. Create only work you have the right to submit. Do not copy third-party characters, artwork, scripts, logos or protected material without authorization.
5. Submit the requested evidence through a pull request, issue comment or other method explicitly named in the challenge.
6. Maintainers review the work against the published acceptance criteria.
7. Accepted work moves to `VERIFIED` and the MYZ reward is recorded in `rewards.json` or the platform reward ledger.
8. A merge, approval or issue close alone does not prove that an external or on-chain payment occurred. Current repository rules define MYZ as an internal reward/accounting unit unless separate settlement evidence exists.

Typical lifecycle:

```text
OPEN
  -> CLAIMED / IN_PROGRESS
  -> SUBMITTED
  -> UNDER_REVIEW
  -> VERIFIED | REJECTED | REVISION_REQUESTED
  -> REWARD_RECORDED
```

## Current reward examples

The initial program includes challenges for:

- **250 MYZ** — comic story concept;
- **500 MYZ** — character sheet;
- **750 MYZ** — storyboard / page layout;
- **1,500 MYZ** — finished multi-page comic sequence;
- **5,000 MYZ** — complete short comic / flagship episode.

Canonical machine-readable challenge definitions are in [`challenges.json`](./challenges.json).

## Originality and rights

Contributors must submit original work or material for which they hold all necessary rights.

Unless a specific bounty says otherwise, acceptance of a rewarded contribution requires the contributor to grant MyZubster a **non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual license** to reproduce, publish, display, distribute and adapt the accepted work for MyZubster project, documentation, promotional and community purposes. The creator retains copyright in their original contribution.

A bounty may request different licensing terms, but those terms must be stated in the issue before work begins.

Contributors remain responsible for ensuring that their submission does not infringe third-party copyrights, trademarks, publicity rights or other rights.

## AI-assisted work

AI-assisted creative work is allowed only when the bounty permits it. Contributors must disclose material AI assistance and remain responsible for rights, originality, consistency and final quality. Do not submit prompts or outputs designed to imitate a living artist or protected franchise in a way that creates avoidable infringement risk.

## Quality review

Creative review may consider:

- adherence to the brief;
- storytelling clarity;
- visual consistency;
- readability and lettering;
- character continuity;
- production quality;
- originality;
- suitability for MyZubster's public communications;
- delivery of required source/export files.

A subjective preference alone should not override explicit acceptance criteria. Review decisions should identify the unmet criterion when revision or rejection is requested.

## Safety and content

Submissions must not include illegal content, non-consensual sexual material, hateful or extremist propaganda, doxxing, private credentials, unnecessary personal data or instructions that create real-world danger.

## MYZ rewards

All rewards in this comics program use **MYZ**. There is no automatic fiat conversion and no silent substitution with XMR or another asset.

See the repository-level [`BOUNTIES.md`](../BOUNTIES.md) for the canonical MyZubster bounty lifecycle, verification and accounting rules.
