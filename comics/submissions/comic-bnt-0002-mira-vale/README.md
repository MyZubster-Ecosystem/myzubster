# Mira Vale Character Sheet

Submission for `COMIC-BNT-0002` / issue #570.

## Character

Mira Vale is a field archivist who helps people understand digital identity,
travel permissions, and community records. She is observant, practical, and
approachable rather than heroic or militarized.

## Redraw Guide

- Silhouette: short wavy hair, cropped utility jacket, straight trousers, and
  a compact cross-body satchel.
- Constant identifiers: one teal hair streak, amber jacket, off-white high
  collar, teal satchel strap, and a round geometric identity pin.
- Proportions: grounded adult proportions with practical footwear and relaxed
  shoulders.
- Expressions: calm, delighted, skeptical, and concerned are supplied on the
  sheet as reusable references.
- The pin is an original abstract mark. It is not the MyZubster logo and should
  not be replaced with a production credential or real identifier.
- Do not add weapons, armor, sexualized styling, or third-party franchise
  elements.
