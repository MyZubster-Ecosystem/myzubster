# MyZubster Bounty Framework

## Civic Pilot Projects (Progetto Pilota Comune)
This framework is designed for testing verifiable bounties to improve accessibility and quality of public services.

### Lifecycle
1. **Proposal**: Submission of problems/proposals related to public services.
2. **Validation**: Criteria and metrics defined ex-ante.
3. **Approval**: Institutional/Authorized subject approval.
4. **Funding**: Treasury/Funding allocation.
5. **Activity**: Execution of the task.
6. **Verification**: Evidence-based verification by an authorized subject.
7. **Settlement**: Reward release after successful verification.
8. **Reporting**: Public/Institutional report compatible with privacy and regulations.

### Scope
- Selection of 3-5 problems/proposals.
- Verifiable evidence required for all tasks.
- Settlement only after verification.
