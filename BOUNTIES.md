# MyZubster Bounties

## Civic Pilot: Urban Decor & Maintenance

**Status:** `PROPOSED`

### Objective
Test the MyZubster ecosystem through a small set of verifiable reports regarding urban decor, maintenance, and public space issues.

### Scope & Workflow
1. **Selection:** 3–5 specific cases.
2. **Bounty:** Defined with clear acceptance criteria.
3. **Evidence:** Collection of photographic/digital evidence.
4. **Verification:** Validation by an authorized subject (e.g., Municipal representative).
5. **Settlement:** Only after successful verification.

### Acceptance Criteria
- Evidence must clearly show the issue and the resolution.
- Verification must be signed off by the authorized entity.
- Settlement occurs via the designated rail (MYZ/XMR/TOKEN) upon verification.
