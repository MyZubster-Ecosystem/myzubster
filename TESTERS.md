# MyZubster — External Tester Call

We are validating one simple public action: **publish a local observation with public evidence in under 10 minutes**.

## Who we need
Independent users who are not MyZubster maintainers and who can submit one real observation from their local environment, public space, garden, mobility context, water point, biodiversity sighting, waste/circularity context or other safe public observation.

## Start
1. Read the 5-minute quickstart: `docs/QUICKSTART_OBSERVATION.md`.
2. Open the public observation form: https://github.com/MyZubster-Ecosystem/myzubster/issues/new/choose
3. Submit one real observation with date, approximate location and public evidence.
4. Record whether this is your first MyZubster action.
5. Record your elapsed-time range and any onboarding friction.

## Target
- Target experience: **5 minutes**.
- Phase 1 maximum acceptable median: **under 10 minutes**.
- Validation target: **10 qualifying first-time external submissions**.

## What counts
A qualifying test must:
- come from a non-maintainer;
- describe a real observation;
- include an observation date;
- include an approximate safe location;
- include public evidence or a clear note explaining evidence awaiting safe publication;
- include the contributor declarations;
- report whether it is the contributor's first MyZubster action;
- report an elapsed-time range.

## What does not count
Founder-only submissions, maintainer-only tests, spam, duplicates, fabricated observations, promotional posts, passive views, stars or unchanged forks.

## Evidence boundary
A submitted observation is evidence that a contributor completed the public observation workflow. It does not by itself prove environmental impact, scientific validity, partnership, endorsement, commercial adoption or deployment of broader MyZubster infrastructure.
