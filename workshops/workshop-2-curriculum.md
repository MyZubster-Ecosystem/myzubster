# Workshop 2: AI for Plant Care

## Detailed Curriculum

**Duration:** 75 minutes  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Prerequisites:** Workshop 1 or basic gardening knowledge

---

## 📚 Learning Objectives

By the end of this workshop, participants will be able to:
1. Understand AI applications in gardening
2. Use plant disease detection tools
3. Implement growth prediction
4. Analyze environmental data with AI

---

## 📝 Workshop Agenda

### 0:00 - 0:15 | AI Basics

**What is Machine Learning?**
- Training data and models
- Supervised vs unsupervised
- Real-world AI examples

**AI in Gardening**
- Disease detection
- Growth prediction
- Pest identification
- Yield optimization

---

### 0:15 - 0:40 | Plant Disease Detection

**How It Works**
1. Image capture
2. Feature extraction
3. Classification
4. Result and recommendations

**Demo: Live Disease Detection**
- Upload plant photo
- See detection results
- View treatment recommendations

**Hands-On Exercise:**
- Upload 5 sample images
- Identify diseases
- Note confidence scores

---

### 0:40 - 1:00 | Growth Prediction

**Predictive Models**
- Growth rate calculation
- Harvest time estimation
- Environmental factor analysis

**Factors Considered**
- Temperature history
- Water usage
- Light exposure
- Soil conditions

**Demo: Growth Prediction Dashboard**
- View predicted harvest dates
- See growth curves
- Adjust variables

---

### 1:00 - 1:15 | Advanced Topics & Q&A

**Advanced AI Applications**
- Automated watering systems
- Companion planting recommendations
- Seasonal planning

**Ethical Considerations**
- Data privacy
- Algorithmic bias
- Accessibility

**Q&A Session**

---

## 🎯 Exercises

### Exercise 1: Disease Identification (15 min)
Given 10 plant images:
1. Identify healthy vs diseased plants
2. Name the disease if present
3. Suggest treatment

### Exercise 2: Growth Prediction (15 min)
Using provided data:
1. Calculate predicted harvest date
2. Identify limiting factors
3. Suggest optimizations

### Exercise 3: AI Tool Comparison (10 min)
Compare two AI tools:
1. Accuracy
2. Ease of use
3. Recommendations quality
