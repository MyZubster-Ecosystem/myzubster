## Counting rules

An IWAU is a unique non-maintainer who completes at least one verifiable MyZubster action during the seven-day window. Examples include a qualifying contribution, independent integration/deployment, independently documented demo run, technical issue arising from actual use, or qualifying observation quickstart.

One person performing several actions in the same week counts once toward IWAU, though the individual actions may count in their respective metrics.

## Weekly update procedure

1. Define the exact seven-day window.
2. Search public MyZubster ecosystem PRs/issues and known third-party evidence.
3. Exclude maintainer-only and non-use signals.
4. Deduplicate people for IWAU.
5. Record only evidence links that can be independently inspected.
6. Use `minimum verified` when the search is not guaranteed exhaustive.
7. Never infer partnership, payment, commercial adoption, production deployment or institutional endorsement without explicit evidence.
