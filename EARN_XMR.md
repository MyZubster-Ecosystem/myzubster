# 💰 Earn Your First XMR

**Your gateway to earning Monero in the MyZubster ecosystem — no capital needed.**

MyZubster is a community-driven project, and we reward contributors who help us grow. Whether you're registering the first plant in your neighborhood, verifying someone else's entry, or fixing a bug — you earn real XMR (Monero) for your efforts.

1. **Pick an action** from the table below
2. **Follow the linked guide** in the relevant repository
3. **Submit your contribution** (PR, issue, or registration)
4. **Get paid in XMR** to your Monero wallet

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Do I need a Monero wallet?**
A: Yes. You can get a free wallet at [getmonero.org](https://www.getmonero.org/downloads/). We recommend the official GUI or Feather wallet.

**Q: How do I get paid?**
A: Include your XMR address in your PR description or issue comment. Payments are processed after merge/verification.

**Q: Can I earn without coding?**
A: Absolutely! Registering plants/animals, reporting bugs, writing docs, and community participation all earn rewards.

**Q: What's the minimum payout?**
A: There's no minimum — every contribution counts.

**Q: Are rewards guaranteed?**
A: Rewards are subject to available funding. Check the [FUNDING.md](https://github.com/MyZubster-Ecosystem/myzubster/blob/main/FUNDING.md) for current status.
