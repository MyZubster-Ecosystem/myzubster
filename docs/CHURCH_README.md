# MyZubster Church — README

> **Status: CONCEPT / DOCUMENTED.** This README defines a possible MyZubster community and metaverse space. It does **not** claim the existence of a real religious institution, denomination, clergy, place of worship or partnership with any faith organisation.

## 1. What is the MyZubster Church?

The working concept of **MyZubster Church** is a symbolic, cultural and community space inside the MyZubster digital world.

Its purpose is to provide a quiet and inclusive environment for:

- reflection;
- remembrance and memorial experiences;
- community gatherings;
- cultural storytelling;
- environmental reflection;
- voluntary ceremonies or symbolic events;
- dialogue about responsibility toward people, animals and the environment.

It should not be presented as MyZubster's official religion.

## 2. Core principle

```text
OPEN COMMUNITY SPACE
        ↓
reflection + memory + culture
        ↓
respect for different beliefs
        ↓
voluntary participation
        ↓
no claim of religious authority
```

The space must remain usable by people with different religions, spiritual traditions or no religious belief.

## 3. What it is not

The MyZubster Church concept is not automatically:

- a legally recognised church;
- a religious denomination;
- a replacement for a physical place of worship;
- a theological authority;
- a provider of sacraments;
- a charity or foundation;
- an organisation authorised to collect religious donations;
- a representation of Christianity, Islam, Judaism, Buddhism, Hinduism or another faith;
- an official partner of the Vatican, a diocese or any religious organisation.

Any future relationship with a real religious body must be documented separately and supported by explicit evidence.

## 4. Place in the MyZubster world

Conceptually:

```text
MyZubster World
 ├── Gardens / environmental spaces
 ├── Community spaces
 ├── Characters
 ├── Knowledge / Chronicle
 ├── DAO / governance spaces
 └── Church / Reflection Space
        ├── Hall of Reflection
        ├── Memorial Garden
        ├── Community Gathering Area
        └── Cultural Archive
```

These names describe possible experience zones, not currently verified deployed features.

## 5. Hall of Reflection

A quiet virtual area could allow a user to:

- enter with their MyZubster character;
- read selected reflections or community messages;
- spend time without gameplay pressure;
- view environmental or cultural visualisations;
- leave the space at any time.

No user should be required to declare a religion to enter.

## 6. Memorial Garden

A future Memorial Garden could provide a respectful digital place for remembrance.

Potential content might include user-authorised memorial cards, trees/plants used as symbolic representations, community-created messages and Chronicle entries.

### Privacy boundary

Memorial features can involve sensitive personal information. Therefore:

- nothing should be published without appropriate authorisation;
- unnecessary personal data must not be collected;
- private grief or family information must not become public by default;
- minors require additional safeguards;
- deletion/moderation processes must exist before public launch.

## 7. Environmental dimension

The Church/Reflection Space can connect symbolically to MyZubster's environmental mission without making scientific or theological claims.

Possible experiences include:

```text
real environmental evidence
        ↓
MyZubster visualisation
        ↓
reflection on stewardship / responsibility
```

Scientific data remains scientific evidence. A reflection or narrative inspired by that evidence is a different layer and must be labelled accordingly.

## 8. Characters

Users could enter the space using their MyZubster character.

The character represents the user's application persona. It must not automatically encode or expose:

- religion;
- political belief;
- health information;
- sexual orientation;
- private family information;
- wallet/private-key information.

Religious or philosophical profile attributes should never be mandatory for participation.

## 9. MyZubster TV

A future TV experience could expose a simple remote-friendly entry point:

```text
MyZubster TV
  ↓
Community
  ↓
Reflection Space
  ↓
[Enter] [Memorial Garden] [Community Event] [Exit]
```

Requirements:

- D-pad navigation;
- visible focus;
- predictable Back behaviour;
- no mouse requirement;
- accessible text sizing;
- optional audio/subtitles where relevant;
- clear exit from every experience.

This is an architectural target, not a claim that the TV route is currently deployed.

## 10. Zorgax

Zorgax could support the space as an informational and storytelling assistant.

Appropriate roles include:

- explain how the space works;
- retrieve approved public Chronicle material;
- distinguish evidence from narrative;
- help structure community documentation;
- moderate according to defined policy where technically implemented;
- present multiple perspectives neutrally when discussing traditions or cultural history.

Zorgax must not present itself as:

- priest, imam, rabbi, monk or other clergy;
- divine authority;
- prophet;
- spiritual diagnostician;
- substitute for a person's faith community;
- source of binding religious rulings.

## 11. DAO relationship

The DAO may eventually help govern platform-level rules for the space, such as moderation standards, technical features or community event formats.

DAO voting must not be used to declare theological truth or decide what an individual is required to believe.

Conceptually:

```text
DAO may govern → platform rules
DAO may not define → mandatory personal belief
```

## 12. Community events

Possible events include:

- environmental remembrance days;
- community reflection sessions;
- cultural storytelling;
- memorial events with appropriate consent;
- discussions about stewardship and sustainability;
- virtual exhibitions;
- guest events with verified organisations.

A guest speaker or event does not establish an institutional partnership unless separately confirmed.

## 13. Real religious organisations

If a church, diocese, university, association or other faith/cultural organisation becomes interested in participating, classify the relationship conservatively:

```text
DISCOVERY
→ INTEREST
→ CONTRIBUTION
→ INTEGRATION
→ VERIFIED_ADOPTION
```

Do not jump from an email, meeting or event invitation to `VERIFIED_ADOPTION` or partnership.

A formal collaboration should record scope, authorised representatives, permitted use of names/logos/content, privacy/data terms and duration where applicable.

## 14. Donations and money

The concept does not currently establish a religious donation system.

Do not label payments, DAO treasury movements, MYZ rewards or marketplace transactions as religious donations unless a legally and technically appropriate mechanism has been explicitly established.

Never expose wallet seed phrases, private keys or payment credentials.

## 15. Content provenance

Every visual/content item should be classified.

### REAL EVIDENCE

Actual photographs, documents, public statements or verified records from a real institution/event.

### DOCUMENTATION_VISUAL

A diagram, UI mock-up or architectural illustration explaining the MyZubster Church concept.

### NARRATIVE_ILLUSTRATION

Artwork depicting the symbolic MyZubster world, characters, sanctuary, garden or ceremony.

Narrative artwork must never be presented as a photograph of a real church, real religious leader or real event.

## 16. Moderation

Before public community interaction is enabled, define policies for:

- harassment;
- hate or attacks based on religion/belief;
- impersonation of clergy/institutions;
- spam/scams;
- extremist propaganda/recruitment;
- graphic content;
- memorial abuse;
- misinformation presented as official institutional statements;
- unauthorised personal information.

Moderation actions should be auditable and appealable where appropriate.

## 17. Inclusivity

The space should follow these principles:

- participation is voluntary;
- no religion is required;
- no user is ranked by belief;
- different beliefs receive equal platform protection under the rules;
- non-belief is equally permitted;
- accessibility is considered from design time;
- community rules prohibit harassment based on belief or non-belief.

## 18. Children and vulnerable users

Any future child-accessible experience requires age-appropriate design, privacy safeguards and moderation.

The platform must not encourage children to disclose private religious, family or personal information publicly.

## 19. Architecture concept

A future implementation could use:

```text
Authenticated MyZubster account
        ↓
Character / profile
        ↓
Reflection Space API
        ↓
approved public content + user-authorised content
        ↓
Web / TV / metaverse experience
        ↓
moderation + provenance + audit boundary
```

No sensitive belief field is required for this architecture.

## 20. Maturity states

Use the standard MyZubster maturity language:

- `CONCEPT` — idea has been defined;
- `DOCUMENTED` — behaviour and boundaries are documented;
- `IMPLEMENTED` — code exists;
- `CI_VERIFIED` — automated checks pass for the relevant commit;
- `DEVICE_VERIFIED` — TV/metaverse experience tested on intended hardware where applicable;
- `DEPLOYED` — available in intended environment;
- `PRODUCTION_READY` — privacy, moderation, security and operational gates passed;
- `ADOPTED` — verified external use exists.

This README moves the concept only to **DOCUMENTED**.

## 21. Suggested first implementation

Keep the first technical slice small:

### MyZubster Reflection Room v0

- one public room;
- no religious affiliation field;
- no payments;
- no private messaging;
- no memorial personal data;
- curated MyZubster environmental/cultural content only;
- D-pad-compatible TV UI;
- clear `DOCUMENTATION / NARRATIVE` labelling;
- exit/back controls;
- basic accessibility.

This creates a testable community space without introducing sensitive data or institutional claims.

## 22. Definition of Done

The MyZubster Church / Reflection Space can be considered production-ready only when:

```text
inclusive concept
  → approved community policy
  → privacy review
  → moderation boundary
  → implemented authenticated experience
  → provenance-labelled content
  → accessibility checks
  → CI verification
  → real-device/user QA
  → deployment
```

Any claim of collaboration with a real religious organisation requires separate, explicit evidence.
