VISUAL #019 · EVIDENCE-FIRST

Contributor Trust / Verification Journey

A contributor can use a public alias or character, submit lawful work, attach evidence and build non-transferable trust through repeatable verification. Rewards remain separate from authority, identity and reputation.

Journey

Each promotion requires evidence appropriate to the next state.

01

Alias / Character

A public Git alias or original MyZubster character can represent a contributor.

Does not prove

Legal identity, employment, partnership, expertise or trust.

02

Scoped Contribution

The contributor chooses lawful work with explicit scope, repository or bounty context.

Required boundary

Security-sensitive work requires written authorization and scope. Out-of-scope activity does not earn trust.

03

Evidence Package

PRs, commits, hashes, reproducible steps or public-safe references document the claimed action.

Evidence rule

A hash proves integrity of referenced bytes, not truth of every underlying claim.

04

Independent Verification

Verifier quorum checks scope, evidence quality, duplicates, conflicts and reproducibility.

Conflict rule

No contributor may self-approve where independent verification is required.

05

Verified Contribution History

Accepted evidence can become part of a persistent contribution history linked to the alias/character.

Meaning

This verifies contribution history, not a government identity or universal competence.

06

Operational Trust

Trust may increase from repeated verified contributions, evidence quality, verifier reliability, policy compliance and dispute history.

Non-transferable

Trust is contextual and cannot be bought, transferred or inferred from token balance.

07

Reward Boundary

An approved contribution may produce a MYZ ledger reward according to policy.

Separate systems

MYZ reward ≠ authority, reputation, external payment or settlement finality.

08

Active Character / Role

A character may visually represent a verified contributor role in the Git-native world after supporting evidence exists.

Visual rule

The character image is narrative/UI. Repository evidence remains the verification layer.

Trust model

  1. 01Verified contribution history
  2. 02Evidence quality
  3. 03Verifier reliability
  4. 04Policy compliance
  5. 05Dispute history

Hard boundaries

Character ≠ identity certification

Aliases and characters are allowed; stronger verification may exist privately for high-value workflows without publishing personal data.

Trust ≠ reward

MYZ can record rewards, but token ownership must never buy Trust Score or governance authority by default.

Trust ≠ security clearance

Past contributions do not authorize access to systems. Scope and authorization are evaluated per task.

Verified ≠ infallible

A verified contribution is bounded to its evidence and decision context; future contradiction or new evidence can require review.

Rule: contributor trust comes from verified lawful contribution and repeatable evidence, not image, notoriety, token balance or self-assertion.
