## 🚀 Step-by-Step: Earn Your First XMR With Zero Capital

### 1. Get a Monero wallet
You need a place to receive XMR. Pick one:
- **[Feather Wallet](https://featherwallet.org/)** — lightweight desktop (recommended)
- **[Cake Wallet](https://cakewallet.com/)** — mobile (iOS / Android)
- Or any wallet from **[getmonero.org/downloads](https://www.getmonero.org/downloads/)**

Copy your **primary address** (starts with `4` or `8`). Keep it private but share it
with maintainers when claiming a bounty.

### 2. Pick your first easy win
Start with a 🟢 Easy action — e.g. **register a plant** in `myzubster` or
**improve documentation**. These need no coding.

### 3. Claim & do the work
On the relevant issue, comment to claim it and **paste your XMR payout address**, then
complete the task and open a PR (use `Closes #ISSUE`).

### 4. Get verified & paid
Once your PR is merged (or your report validated), the maintainer processes the XMR
payout. Track status in **[docs/PAYMENTS.md](PAYMENTS.md)**.

> 💡 **First time?** Documentation and plant/animal registration are the fastest path
> to your first `0.001–0.002 XMR`.

## 📌 How Payouts Work

- Rewards are offered in **XMR** and paid to the address you provide when claiming.
- Payment is processed after your contribution is **merged / validated**.
- Live status (paid / pending / in review) is tracked in
  **[docs/PAYMENTS.md](PAYMENTS.md)**.
- Some bounties may be temporarily **pending funding** — the maintainer will note this
  on the issue. Contributions are still credited and paid once funds are available.
