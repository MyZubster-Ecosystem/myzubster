## Recommended naming convention
- `utm_source`: origin or partner, e.g. `facebook`, `enea`, `university`, `github`
- `utm_medium`: channel type, e.g. `social`, `institutional`, `research`, `developer`
- `utm_campaign`: initiative, e.g. `life-2027`, `circular-water`, `zorgax-community`

When a specific partner gets a unique link, replace `utm_source` with a short lowercase identifier such as `polimi`, `unicusano`, `irsa-cnr`, `hera` or `crpa` so visits can be attributed more precisely.
