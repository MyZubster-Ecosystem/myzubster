## Recommended naming convention
- `utm_source`: origin or partner, e.g. `facebook`, `enea`, `polimi`, `unicusano`, `irsa-cnr`, `crpa`, `hera`, `unitelma`, `unibs`, `github`
- `utm_medium`: channel type, e.g. `social`, `institutional`, `research`, `partner`, `industrial`, `developer`
- `utm_campaign`: initiative, e.g. `life-2027`, `circular-water`, `lca-lcsa`, `pilot-roadmap`, `circular-economy-mrv`, `zorgax-community`

Use one stable source identifier per organization. Do not reuse a partner-specific link for another organization, otherwise attribution becomes ambiguous.
