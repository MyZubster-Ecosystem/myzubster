# LIFE 2026 — Real-world stakeholder characters

These characters are grounded in real roles required by the MyZubster LIFE 2026 workstream, but they are **not portraits, impersonations, endorsements, or official representations of specific people or organisations**.

They exist to make the LIFE workflow understandable inside MyZubster while keeping private correspondence, personal data, unconfirmed partnerships, and negotiation status out of the public repository.

## Character set

### 1. The Scientific Coordinator
**Mission:** keep the environmental methodology defensible and measurable.

- Works on hydraulics, water systems, climate resilience and scientific validation.
- Reviews baselines, KPI definitions, QA/QC and uncertainty.
- Helps translate the pilot into evidence suitable for LIFE reporting.
- Can support consortium coordination when backed by an institution with LIFE administrative capacity.

**MyZubster interactions:** validate KPI methodology, approve evidence rules, review data quality, sign off technical milestones.

### 2. The Water Data Steward
**Mission:** connect operational water data to the environmental evidence layer without exposing restricted information.

- Represents the role of a utility, authority or infrastructure operator.
- Defines which datasets can be shared, at what granularity and for which purpose.
- Guards privacy, cybersecurity, operational confidentiality and data-use constraints.
- Supports baseline construction and technical validation.

**MyZubster interactions:** register data sources, approve schemas, define access rules, validate provenance and update frequency.

### 3. The Technical Data Validator
**Mission:** turn domain measurements into usable, auditable environmental indicators.

- Brings real technical values from water, sludge, nutrients, organic matrices or circular-economy processes.
- Helps interpret measurements and define practical KPI thresholds.
- Supports demonstration cases with operational knowledge.
- Bridges field data and digital MRV.

**MyZubster interactions:** map raw measurements to evidence records, validate units and ranges, annotate anomalies, review pilot outputs.

### 4. The Replication & Policy Lead
**Mission:** make the demonstrator transferable beyond one site.

- Coordinates replication methodology and policy uptake.
- Engages territories, public actors, innovation ecosystems and stakeholders.
- Converts project results into reusable governance and implementation patterns.
- Tracks replication commitments and post-project adoption.

**MyZubster interactions:** create replication cases, maintain stakeholder maps, track uptake milestones, package reusable implementation guidance.

### 5. The Pilot Operator
**Mission:** prove the system works in a real operational context.

- Hosts or supports a field demonstration.
- Provides operational context, workflows, constraints and measurable before/after conditions.
- Works with the data steward and validator to define the monitoring perimeter.
- Reports practical usability and deployment issues.

**MyZubster interactions:** register assets, log observations, connect field evidence, confirm operational milestones and validate corrective actions.

### 6. The Mediterranean Replication Partner
**Mission:** test whether the model transfers across territories and operating conditions.

- Adapts circular-water and environmental-MRV methods to a second context.
- Supports stakeholder engagement, exploitation, transferability and replication.
- Challenges assumptions that only work in the flagship territory.

**MyZubster interactions:** create a replication workspace, compare baseline/KPI models, document adaptation requirements and transferability evidence.

## Status model

Each character may have one of these public-safe statuses inside MyZubster:

- `ROLE_DEFINED` — the role exists in the project architecture.
- `CANDIDATE` — an organisation may be suitable, but no commitment is claimed.
- `IN_DISCUSSION` — active dialogue exists; no partnership is implied.
- `CONFIRMED` — use only when written confirmation/LoI exists and publication is authorised.
- `INACTIVE` — role/contact is no longer being pursued.

## Privacy and representation rule

Do not attach a real person's name, photo, email, phone number, private message, or likeness to these characters unless there is explicit permission for public representation. Organisation names and commitment status must also be updated only from publishable, authorised evidence.
