## Verified facts

1. **Both Nature SAP topics currently under review are open and have the same deadline.**
   - `LIFE-2026-SAP-NAT-GOV` — Standard Action Projects for Nature and Biodiversity — Governance and Information.
   - `LIFE-2026-SAP-NAT-NATURE` — Standard Action Projects for Nature and Biodiversity — Nature.
   - CINEA lists both as open, single-stage calls with a deadline of **22 September 2026, 17:00 CEST**.

2. **The applicant eligibility gate requires a legal organisation/entity; individuals are not eligible applicants.**
   - CINEA's applicant-support page states that eligible applicants include public or private legal entities registered in the EU or an eligible associated-country context, and explicitly states that natural persons are not eligible to apply.
   - The LIFE 2026 FAQ likewise states that a proposal may be submitted by a public or private organisation legally registered in the EU (including OCTs) or a country associated to LIFE, subject to the call document's eligibility provisions.

3. **For SAP Nature projects, the LIFE 2026 FAQ identifies an After-LIFE Conservation Plan as a required project deliverable.**
   - FAQ section 1.1, Q.25 states that the required deliverable for **all SAP Nature projects** is an **After-LIFE Conservation Plan**.
   - The same FAQ describes this plan as setting out how the project will continue after LIFE funding and what actions are needed to consolidate results and support favourable conservation status / biodiversity outcomes.

4. **Application submission is through the Funding & Tenders Portal.**
   - CINEA's applicant-support page states that applications must be submitted through the Funding & Tenders Portal.

5. **The general LIFE 2026 Calls FAQ was updated on 15 July 2026.**
   - CINEA currently labels the general LIFE 2026 Calls FAQ as updated on `15/07/2026`.

## Implications for the MyZubster LIFE working track

- The current working deadline of **22 September 2026, 17:00 CEST** for the two Nature SAP options remains supported by official CINEA evidence.
- The applicant question is a **gating item**: the project cannot rely on an individual applicant. The specific MyZubster legal entity, its registration status, eligibility and Participant Register/PIC readiness remain to be verified before submission.
- Because both `NAT-GOV` and `NAT-NATURE` are SAP Nature topics, the working package for sustainability/replication should explicitly include preparation of the **After-LIFE Conservation Plan** required by the FAQ.
- The technology stack remains an enabling mechanism. The After-LIFE plan and the proposal overall must be framed around continuation and consolidation of measurable biodiversity/conservation outcomes, not around continuation of the software platform as an end in itself.
