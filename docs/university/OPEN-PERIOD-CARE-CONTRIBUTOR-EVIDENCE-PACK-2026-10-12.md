## What can be demonstrated in the meeting

1. **External contribution is real and inspectable**  
   Repository, branch, PR and commit evidence can be opened publicly.

2. **Research is converted into structured evidence**  
   The Open Period Care work is being mapped into a machine-readable Circular Care payload.

3. **Contributor knowledge remains attributable**  
   The project keeps contributor identity, evidence source, evidence state and project provenance separate.

4. **Zorgax can navigate the evidence path**  
   The objective is to let Zorgax help users inspect sources, Knowledge Cards, contributor provenance and project evidence without silently upgrading claims.

5. **Independent nodes can interoperate through MyZubster**  
   The Research Bridge (#1488) defines a controlled Docker/VPS path for researcher, reviewer and future academic-review packages.

6. **Optional blockchain anchoring is evidence integrity, not scientific proof**  
   SHA-256 / testnet anchoring may timestamp a canonical payload, but does not by itself prove physical recycling, product safety, clinical validity, professional qualification or institutional endorsement.

## Current review status

PR #1489 is **not yet accepted as-is**. Maintainer review requested corrections before merge, including:
- use `SUPPORTED` rather than `VERIFIED` where independent validation is absent;
- avoid claiming scientific peer review without evidence;
- avoid claiming ISO compliance without validation;
- distinguish Git commit identifiers from SHA-256 hashes;
- do not present a blockchain anchor as confirmed until a public receipt/verifier proves the exact payload was anchored.

This review process is itself part of the MyZubster model: **claims are separated from evidence and are corrected before becoming canonical project history.**

## University / research bridge

A future academic reviewer could receive a sanitized package containing:
- public source list;
- evidence matrix;
- open questions;
- current evidence state;
- version/hash;
- links to repository evidence.

This document does **not** claim that the university, CIRI or any institution has formally joined or endorsed the Open Period Care / Circular Care pilot. Any academic participation must be confirmed separately.
