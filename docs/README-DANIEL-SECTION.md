## 👤 Daniel Ioni — Founder, Builder & Connected Ecosystem

Daniel Ioni ([`DanielIoni-creator`](https://github.com/DanielIoni-creator)) is the creator and lead builder of **MyZubster**, an open digital ecosystem connecting open-source development, community communication, immersive spaces, evidence-aware cultural documentation and LIFE-oriented initiatives.

### 🔗 Shared channel direction

```text
FACEBOOK / MESSENGER ─┐
TELEGRAM              ├──→ MYZUBSTER CHANNEL LAYER ─→ ZORGAX
WEB / FUMETTO         ┤                  │
GITHUB                ┘                  ├──→ METAVERSE
                                         ├──→ MARKETPLACE
                                         ├──→ LIFE / PROJECTS
                                         ├──→ CULTURE / SUBCULTURE
                                         └──→ PUBLIC DOCUMENTATION
```

The goal is not to duplicate AI/business logic for every social network. Channel-specific adapters should handle transport while shared MyZubster services preserve canonical routes, application truth and evidence boundaries.

### 🎵 Culture, Subculture & real-world continuity

```text
REAL-WORLD EVENT / ACTIVITY
        ↓
PEOPLE · MUSIC · ART · COMMUNITY
        ↓
MESSENGER / TELEGRAM
        ↓
ZORGAX
        ↓
MYZUBSTER
   ├── CULTURE / SUBCULTURE
   ├── METAVERSE
   ├── LIFE
   └── COMMUNITY / DOCUMENTATION
        ↓
FUTURE AUTHORIZED REAL-WORLD ACTIVITY
```

The digital layer is intended to preserve and extend connections created in the real world, not replace them. Authentication, testimony, historical verification, relationship confirmation and collective authorization remain separate concepts; a social account never proves membership in or authority to represent an external collective.
