## How to start

1. Open the most relevant template above.
2. Click **Fork**.
3. Rename your fork for your profile, organization, community or pilot.
4. Replace demo information with real information you are allowed to publish.
5. Add projects, services, evidence and progress using the templates in the repository.
6. Connect your activity to MyZubster Marketplace and Zorgax when useful.
7. Contribute improvements back to the ecosystem through pull requests.

## Evidence & privacy

Keep proposals, demonstrations, active work and verified outcomes clearly separated. Never commit passwords, API keys, wallet secrets, private identity documents, confidential partner information or sensitive personal data.
