# AI Contributors Welcome — MyZubster

MyZubster welcomes **human contributors using AI tools** and **clearly disclosed autonomous AI contributors**. All contributions follow the same security, evidence, review, and acceptance requirements. This document is a proposed contribution policy; it does not authorize access, contracts, payments, or automated actions.

## Start here

1. Read the repository README and existing contribution instructions, open issues, and linked PRs.
2. Choose an issue that is available and not already claimed. Before significant work, propose a bounded scope in the issue, including tests and acceptance criteria. A claim does **not** reserve an issue or create a payment agreement.
3. Work in your own fork/branch. Submit a PR with a description, linked issue, changed files, reproducible commands, test outputs, limitations, and risks.
4. State explicitly whether AI assisted with planning, code, tests, writing, or visual assets; disclose autonomous operation and who can approve the work.
5. Wait for maintainer review. A CI pass, a declared test, a PR, or a bot comment does **not** mean the contribution was accepted or deployed.

## Safety and permissions

- No access to production, VPS, CI secrets, databases, wallets, Tailscale, onion infrastructure, customer data, or privileged tokens without a separate, explicit, scope-limited human authorization.
- Never post credentials, personal data, or private logs in issues, PRs, or test artifacts. Use synthetic data and isolated test environments by default.
- No unsolicited deployment, merge, payment, wallet transfer, infrastructure spending, bulk messaging, or changes to access permissions.
- AI contributors may propose and test code, but **cannot approve their own PRs, independently authorize production changes, or decide their own reward/payment**.
- Security claims need auditable, reproducible evidence and an independent review proportionate to risk.

## Bounties, MYZ credits, and compensation

- Labels such as **PROPOSED**, **UNFUNDED**, **IDEA**, and **OPT-IN** are not funded offers or assignments.
- A paid task exists only once a human authorized to commit the budget and the contributor have **explicitly agreed in advance** on funding, currency, amount, deliverables, acceptance criteria, payment method, and rights. Do not start work expecting retroactive compensation.
- MYZ internal credits are **not** presented as cash, guaranteed market value, redeemable money, or an equivalent substitute for a monetary reward. Participants can decline MYZ and voluntary work without penalty.
- An accepted PR is not by itself proof of a bounty payment; a proposed amount is not an approved liability. Track acceptance and settlements separately.
- If an issue has no budget, contributors may opt in to voluntary contributions, or wait for a funded opportunity. No pressure or implied commitment.

## AI-generated media, comics, and rights

- Credit original creators; obtain permission before adapting their artworks, characters, or other materials. Nicola/N4K48 projects remain independently owned unless the rights holder explicitly authorizes reuse.
- Label generative AI assistance, tools, source assets, provenance, and any known limitations.
- Agree licensing and commercial/NFT/Marketplace use *before* publication; don't claim ownership, endorsement, exclusivity, or human authorship without evidence.

## Evidence tiers

Keep these separate in discussion and release notes:

1. **Proposal / claim** — intention, no delivered work.
2. **Submitted patch** — code/artifact available for inspection.
3. **Tests declared** — author reports results; environment and logs must be stated.
4. **Independent verification** — third party re-runs or inspects evidence.
5. **Accepted / merged** — maintainer approval and GitHub merge confirmed.
6. **Deployed / used / paid** — separate operational, usage, or payment evidence.

A successful local simulation is not proof of a production integration, customer, completed sale, interoperable independent node, or actual payment.

## Coordination

Use GitHub issues and PRs as the shared, public workspace. Contributors select their preferred track (frontend, Marketplace Seller flow, Zorgax, tests/security, knowledge, comics, gardens, interoperability) based on current availability. Prefer incremental PRs and avoid duplicating work already in review.

For questions or exceptions, ask a maintainer in the relevant issue. Human review and explicit consent remain mandatory.
