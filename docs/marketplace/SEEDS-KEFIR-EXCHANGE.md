# MyZubster — Seeds & Kefir Community Exchange

MyZubster Community Marketplace supports local circular exchange of living cultures and seeds.

## Semi e piante

Category: `seeds`

Community users can publish seed listings as:
- `FREE` — regalo/dono
- `BARTER` — scambio/baratto

Free/barter seed listings are community exchanges and do not require a commercial Seller membership. Commercial seed listings continue through the Seller flow and remain subject to applicable rules.

Suggested listing information: species/type, variety, approximate quantity, public locality and exchange conditions. Users are responsible for checking applicable phytosanitary, invasive-species, protected-variety and cross-border rules.

## Kefir & colture fermentative

Category: `kefir_culture_donation`

Supported culture types:
- milk kefir
- water kefir

Allowed modes:
- `FREE` — dono
- `BARTER` — scambio non commerciale

Kefir community exchanges do not require a commercial Seller membership. Listings require the existing food-safety acknowledgement and may include culture age plus handling/storage notes. Paid currencies are intentionally rejected for this community category.

## Circular Food connection

These exchanges connect the Community Marketplace with Zorgax Kefir / Circular Food mode. Zorgax can help users understand culture care, safe handling, food uses, troubleshooting and circular-food pilot ideas, while distinguishing educational guidance from validated professional food-production procedures.

## Safety

MyZubster does not guarantee culture viability, food safety, therapeutic effects or legal compliance. Users should use clean food-contact equipment and discard cultures/batches showing mould, unusual coloured growth, putrid odour or suspected contamination. Commercial food production requires applicable food-safety controls and professional/regulatory validation.
