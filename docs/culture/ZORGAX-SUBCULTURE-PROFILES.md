# Zorgax Subculture Profiles & Attribution

## Purpose

Zorgax can help authenticated MyZubster contributors build living, evidence-aware profiles of sound systems, tribes, scenes and other subcultures.

The goal is cultural preservation and contributor-led knowledge, not automated claims of membership, authority or historical truth.

## Contributor flow

1. A person signs in to MyZubster through a supported account provider.
2. The person creates or updates a cultural contributor profile in their own capacity.
3. They select or propose a scene, sound system, tribe, movement or subculture.
4. They may contribute memories, dates, places, descriptions, links and images they are entitled to share.
5. Every contribution records its provenance and attribution status.
6. Zorgax helps structure the material, identify gaps, suggest neutral wording and connect related records.
7. The contributor reviews and explicitly approves material before publication.

Social authentication proves control of the authenticated account. It does **not** by itself prove historical membership, employment, authorship, collective representation or organizational authority.

## Evidence and attribution states

Every material claim should carry one of these states:

- `SELF_ATTESTED` — stated by the authenticated contributor about their own experience.
- `CONTRIBUTOR_SUBMITTED` — supplied by a contributor but not independently corroborated.
- `SOURCE_SUPPORTED` — accompanied by a source suitable for review.
- `COLLECTIVE_CONFIRMED` — confirmed through an explicitly authorized collective representative/process.
- `DISPUTED` — conflicting testimony or evidence exists.
- `UNVERIFIED` — insufficient provenance to make a stronger claim.

Zorgax must never silently promote one state into a stronger state.

## Images and media

Contributors may propose photographs of sound systems, events, artwork, flyers, people or other cultural material only when they have a reasonable right or permission to share them.

Each image record should preserve:

- uploader/account provenance;
- original creator/photographer when known;
- approximate date/place when known;
- description and cultural context;
- rights/permission declaration;
- attribution preference;
- verification state;
- moderation/removal status.

Zorgax may describe, organize and connect uploaded material but must not infer a real person's identity from a photograph or use facial recognition to establish cultural membership.

## Subculture graph

Profiles are not limited to Spiral Tribe/SP23. The same neutral structure can represent independent sound systems, free-party scenes, teknival communities, punk/DIY networks, maker cultures, art collectives and other contributor-defined subcultures.

A profile may contain:

- names and aliases;
- time periods and geography;
- cultural principles and practices;
- sound/art/technology characteristics;
- events and places;
- related collectives and influences;
- contributor testimonies;
- media with provenance;
- public sources;
- disagreements/alternative accounts;
- links to MyZubster Cultural Origin Chronicle entries where relevant.

Relationships such as `influenced_by`, `worked_with`, `participated_in`, `related_scene`, `shared_event` and `member_of` must retain their evidence state. `member_of` and representation claims require explicit contributor attestation and must not be inferred by Zorgax.

## Zorgax completion rules

“Complete the subculture” means improve the structure of the record, never invent missing history.

Zorgax may:

- ask contributors for missing dates, places, context and attribution;
- suggest related topics and records;
- summarize multiple approved contributions;
- flag contradictions and provenance gaps;
- propose descriptions clearly labelled as drafts;
- create a chronological cultural map from approved evidence.

Zorgax must not:

- fabricate missing people, events, affiliations or dates;
- turn social login into proof of membership;
- claim that an individual represents a collective without authorization;
- publish private conversations or private images without appropriate permission;
- remove disagreement merely to produce a cleaner narrative.

## Cultural contributor profile

A contributor profile should distinguish:

- authenticated account identity;
- public display name/alias;
- self-attested cultural connections;
- separately verified claims;
- approved contributions;
- attribution/credit preferences;
- consent and withdrawal status.

No Cultural Contributor or Cultural Guardian role automatically grants access to payments, private user data, deployment, security controls or administration.

## Relationship to the September 2026 dialogue

The existing DIY cultural dialogue is an initial use case for this framework. It remains an individual cultural exchange and does not establish Spiral Tribe/SP23 or another collective as a MyZubster partner or endorser.

The framework is deliberately reusable so future authenticated contributors from many subcultures can add their own histories without forcing those histories into a single institutional narrative.

## Principle

**People provide testimony. Sources provide evidence. Authentication provides account provenance. Zorgax connects and organizes them without pretending they are the same thing.**
