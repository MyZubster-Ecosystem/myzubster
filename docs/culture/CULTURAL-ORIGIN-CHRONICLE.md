# MyZubster Cultural Origin Chronicle

## Purpose

MyZubster is developed in public as software, but its history is also human. This chronicle provides a version-controlled record of cultural influences, personal experiences and dialogues that materially shape the project's principles.

The goal is **provenance, not mythology**. Git history can preserve when an idea entered the project and how it was described, while keeping a strict boundary between personal testimony, independently verifiable facts and formal organizational relationships.

## Evidence model

Entries should be classified as one of the following:

- **Project record** — a change, decision or artifact verifiable in the MyZubster repository.
- **Personal testimony** — something a participant says about their own experience. It is recorded as their claim, not automatically as independently verified history.
- **Cultural influence** — an idea, practice or movement that informs MyZubster's direction without implying affiliation.
- **Formal collaboration** — only where an explicit agreement, accepted role or other suitable evidence exists.

Private messages, screenshots, personal details and attributed quotations must not be published merely to strengthen a historical claim. Public attribution requires an appropriate permission basis.

## September 2026 — DIY culture and independent infrastructure

A personal dialogue around MyZubster introduced a documented connection between the project's evolving philosophy and lived experience from the historical DIY sound-system scene. The participant described personal sound-system engineering experience and work around people associated with the Spiral Tribe, Mutoid Waste and Bedlam scene.

The exchange explored a conceptual continuity:

**independent sound systems -> independent digital systems**

The relevant principles for MyZubster are community participation, independence, openness, resilience, the ability to build and modify shared infrastructure, and preservation of cultural memory.

This is a **cultural and personal connection**, not an organizational one. Nothing in this record establishes that Spiral Tribe/SP23, Mutoid Waste, Bedlam or any related collective endorses, represents, governs or is formally partnered with MyZubster.

The underlying dialogue is documented separately in [`DIY-CULTURAL-DIALOGUE.md`](./DIY-CULTURAL-DIALOGUE.md).

## Daniel Ioni and project provenance

MyZubster's development history may include relevant experiences from Daniel Ioni's personal life when those experiences materially explain why a project principle, cultural direction or design choice emerged.

This does not make the repository a general archive of private life. Personal material should enter the public project history only when it has a clear relationship to MyZubster and can be documented without unnecessarily exposing another person's private information.

This distinction allows MyZubster to preserve authentic origin history without converting private relationships into claims of institutional legitimacy.

## Governance principle

Cultural influence does not grant technical or administrative authority. A future Cultural Guardian, historian or advisor role should be advisory by default and should not automatically provide access to payments, user data, deployments, security controls or administration.

No participant should be described as representing a collective or organization unless that representation is separately established.

## Living chronicle

Future entries can record meaningful cultural milestones, collaborations and influences through normal GitHub review. Each addition should identify what kind of evidence it represents and avoid upgrading a personal conversation into a stronger claim than the available evidence supports.

In this way, the repository can preserve both **how MyZubster is built** and **why it came to be built this way**.
