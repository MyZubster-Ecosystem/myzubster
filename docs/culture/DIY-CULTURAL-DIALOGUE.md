# MyZubster DIY Cultural Dialogue

## Status

This document records an early cultural dialogue around MyZubster. It is **not evidence of an official affiliation, endorsement, partnership, or representation by Spiral Tribe/SP23, Mutoid Waste, Bedlam, or any related collective**.

Private screenshots are intentionally not published here.

## Context

In September 2026, Daniel Ioni discussed the open-source and decentralized philosophy of MyZubster with a personal contact who stated in private conversation that he had worked as a sound-system engineer and had worked with people from the Spiral Tribe, Mutoid Waste and Bedlam scene.

The discussion explored parallels between historical DIY sound-system culture and MyZubster's attempt to build open digital infrastructure: community participation, independence, open-source development, resilience, cultural preservation and the ability for people to build rather than remain passive users of centralized systems.

The contact expressed personal encouragement toward the direction being described. This is recorded as a **personal cultural exchange**, not as a statement on behalf of any collective.

## Why MyZubster records this

MyZubster is interested in preserving the human and cultural context around its development, while keeping historical claims precise. The project sees a useful conceptual bridge between independent physical infrastructure such as DIY sound systems and contemporary open digital infrastructure that communities can inspect, fork, adapt and rebuild.

The purpose is not to appropriate names, symbols or histories. It is to learn from people with lived experience, preserve cultural memory where permission exists, and allow those perspectives to inform the project's evolving cultural principles.

## Collaboration and consent boundary

The exchange may be considered an **early collaborative dialogue** in the ordinary sense that ideas and perspectives were shared. It must not be described as a formal collaboration, institutional partnership, endorsement, membership, or authorization from Spiral Tribe/SP23 or any other collective.

No private messages, screenshots, personal biographical details, or attributed quotations should be added to this public repository without the relevant person's explicit permission.

If a participant later explicitly opts in, MyZubster may document their contribution under a mutually agreed attribution and role, including a possible cultural-advisor or Cultural Guardian framework. Such a role would represent that individual's own experience and judgement, never another organization without separate authorization.

## Principle

> From independent sound systems to independent digital systems: preserve the DIY lesson without claiming ownership of its history.

This record is intended to evolve only as additional information and permissions are obtained.
