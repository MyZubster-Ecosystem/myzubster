# Open Source Religion & Belief Framework

> **Belief is personal. Knowledge can be open.**

MyZubster does **not** claim that religions themselves are open source, nor does it claim ownership or authority over any religion, faith, spiritual tradition, philosophy or community.

Instead, MyZubster provides an **open-source technological framework** that can support respectful representation, study, discussion and digital experiences around religion, spirituality, philosophy, atheism, agnosticism and other systems of thought.

## What is open source?

The open-source layer may include:

- software and source code;
- APIs and interoperable formats;
- original educational content released under compatible open licences;
- datasets where copyright, privacy and other applicable rights permit redistribution;
- virtual worlds and cultural spaces;
- community-created modules;
- translations and accessibility tools where licensing permits;
- community contributions, corrections and technical improvements.

Developers may fork eligible MyZubster technology and open content, improve it, build new experiences and contribute changes back to the ecosystem under the applicable licences.

## What is not open source?

Religions themselves are **not software**.

MyZubster does not "open-source" Christianity, Islam, Judaism, Hinduism, Buddhism, Sikhism, Indigenous traditions, atheism or any other belief system.

Sacred texts, translations, artwork, symbols, trademarks, archives and third-party materials retain their respective copyright, licensing, cultural, attribution and other applicable requirements. Inclusion in a MyZubster experience does not change those rights.

Only material that is legally and culturally appropriate for redistribution under the applicable licence should be distributed as open content.

## Neutral infrastructure

MyZubster is infrastructure, not a religious authority.

The platform does not decide:

- which religion or worldview is true;
- which belief is superior;
- what somebody should believe;
- who officially represents a religious community.

The framework should be capable of supporting religious, spiritual, philosophical, atheist and agnostic perspectives without presenting MyZubster as an authority over them.

## Fork the technology, not people's identity

Eligible MyZubster software and openly licensed original content can be forked.

Communities and developers may use the framework to create educational environments, digital museums, virtual cultural spaces, philosophical communities, metaverse experiences, archives and experimental systems of thought.

A fork must not falsely present itself as the official representation of an existing religion, culture or community, and it must continue to respect applicable rights, licences and permissions.

## Core principle

**Freedom of code. Freedom of knowledge. Freedom of belief.**

MyZubster aims to provide open digital infrastructure where technology, culture, philosophy and spirituality can coexist while respecting individual freedom, communities, original sources, intellectual property, privacy and applicable law.
