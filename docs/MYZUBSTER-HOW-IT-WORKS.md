# MyZubster Ecosystem - How It Works

## Marketplace Economics

The MyZubster Marketplace operates on a transparent commission model to ensure ecosystem sustainability.

### Platform Commission
A **2% platform commission** is applied to all eligible real paid Marketplace transactions. 
- **Purpose:** This commission is dedicated to the MyZubster Treasury to cover infrastructure, security, and ecosystem growth.
- **Scope:** This is a platform-level fee and is distinct from any individual operator or founder compensation.

### Founder/Operator Compensation
Any compensation directed to the founding team or specific operators (e.g., a separate 2% share) is **not** part of the standard platform commission. Such compensation must be explicitly documented in the Governance Policy and approved via the DAO treasury model. Until such a policy is canonicalized, the 2% platform commission remains strictly for ecosystem operational costs.

## DAO & MYZ Token Utility
The MYZ token serves as a reward mechanism for verified contributions. It does not inherently grant equity, legal ownership, or guaranteed profit sharing.
