# Open Knowledge Domains Framework

> **Open tools. Open knowledge where rights permit. Human dignity, safety and evidence first.**

MyZubster can provide open-source technological infrastructure across many areas of human knowledge. This does **not** mean that people, identities, scientific facts, cultures, bodies, substances, medical treatments or regulated activities themselves become "open source".

The open layer is the software, schemas, APIs, educational resources, simulations, datasets and community-created material that can legally and responsibly be released under open licences.

## Chemistry

MyZubster may support open software, molecular-data schemas, educational visualizations, simulations, public scientific datasets and reproducible research workflows.

Chemistry remains an evidence-based scientific discipline. Open-source status does not make a chemical safe, legal or appropriate to manufacture or use. Hazardous, controlled or regulated substances and procedures remain subject to applicable safety rules, professional standards and law. Documentation should distinguish educational/research information from operational instructions that could create material harm.

## Sexuality, relationships and sexual health

MyZubster may support respectful educational technology and openly licensed resources concerning human sexuality, relationships, identity, consent, reproductive and sexual health, history and culture.

People and their sexuality are **not datasets to be owned or forked**. Systems must prioritize dignity, privacy, consent, age-appropriate safeguards, non-discrimination and applicable law. Sensitive personal information should not become public merely because the underlying software is open source. Medical or health-related material should identify its evidence and limitations and should not impersonate professional diagnosis or care.

## Biology, health and medicine

Open components may include software, public research data, models, educational material and reproducible analytical workflows where licences, privacy and ethics permit.

Open-source software is not medical approval. Clinical decisions, diagnosis, treatment, human-subject research, genetic information and regulated medical products remain subject to qualified oversight, evidence, privacy requirements and applicable regulation.

## Cannabis and other regulated domains

MyZubster may host lawful educational, historical, scientific, environmental or policy-oriented open-source tooling concerning cannabis or other regulated subjects.

Open-source status does not override age restrictions, controlled-substance law, licensing, health requirements or jurisdiction-specific regulation. Software documentation must not represent a regulated activity as automatically authorized simply because the underlying code is public.

## Environment and circular economy

MyZubster may openly develop tools for environmental observations, waste reduction, recycling, reuse, material tracking, circular-economy research and evidence workflows.

Open data should preserve provenance and distinguish measurements from estimates or hypotheses. Waste handling, biological materials, hazardous materials and industrial processes remain subject to relevant health, environmental and operational rules.

## Culture, philosophy, religion and belief

MyZubster can provide open technological infrastructure for cultural, philosophical, religious, spiritual, atheist and agnostic experiences. Communities, identities and belief systems themselves are not owned by MyZubster. See `OPEN-SOURCE-RELIGION-BELIEF-FRAMEWORK.md`.

## Education

Educational software, curricula created by contributors, simulations and learning tools may be released openly. Third-party books, papers, images, videos and course materials retain their respective rights. Open-source infrastructure does not automatically make every source used by an educational experience freely redistributable.

## Art, media and creative work

MyZubster may support open creative tools and contributor-created media under explicit licences. Copyright, attribution, personality rights, trademarks and third-party licences remain applicable. AI-generated or transformed material should not be represented as authentic historical evidence or as an official work of a third party when it is not.

## Economics, finance and payments

Open-source software may model economic systems, accounting, marketplaces, payments and digital assets. Public code does not itself constitute financial authorization, a guarantee of value, investment advice or proof that a payment occurred. Regulated financial functionality requires its own legal, security and operational assessment.

## AI, robotics and automation

Models, agents, robotics software and orchestration components may be developed openly where licensing permits. Automation should expose provenance, uncertainty and relevant human-review boundaries. Open-source status does not remove responsibility for safety, privacy, cybersecurity or consequential decisions.

## History, politics and public knowledge

Open infrastructure may organize public sources, archives, timelines and educational material. MyZubster should distinguish sourced facts, interpretations, opinions and simulations; preserve citations where possible; and avoid presenting the platform as an official authority merely because information is publicly accessible.

## Universal rule

Across all domains:

```text
DOMAIN / HUMAN ACTIVITY
        !=
OPEN SOURCE PROPERTY

OPEN-SOURCE SOFTWARE + ELIGIBLE OPEN DATA/CONTENT
        ↓
ATTRIBUTION + PROVENANCE + CONSENT + SAFETY
        ↓
APPLICABLE RIGHTS / LICENCES / REGULATION
        ↓
RESPONSIBLE REUSE, FORK AND COLLABORATION
```

### What can be forked

- MyZubster source code under its applicable licence;
- open schemas and APIs;
- contributor-created material explicitly released under compatible licences;
- eligible public/open datasets according to their terms;
- simulations, modules and digital environments where rights permit.

### What cannot be assumed to be forkable

- a person's identity, sexuality, health information or private data;
- religions, cultures or communities themselves;
- copyrighted or culturally restricted third-party material;
- confidential information;
- regulated permissions, licences or professional authority;
- scientific truth or evidence simply by changing software;
- third-party brands, endorsements or institutional authority.

## Core principle

**Freedom of code. Freedom of knowledge. Freedom of identity and belief. Responsibility in application.**

MyZubster's goal is an open digital infrastructure for exploring and building across disciplines while maintaining a clear boundary between what can be openly licensed and the people, rights, evidence, safety requirements and laws that must remain protected.
