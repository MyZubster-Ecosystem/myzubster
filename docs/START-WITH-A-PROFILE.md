# Start with a MyZubster profile

MyZubster supports forkable profiles and pilot repositories for different kinds of participants.

**Choose your path:** [Open the complete Profile & Pilot Template Catalog](PROFILE-TEMPLATE-CATALOG.md).

Typical paths include students, universities and research labs; LIFE-oriented pilots, agriculture, circular water and fermentation/kefir; soundsystems, event production, artists and makers; local services, animal care, developer support and community governance.

## 5-minute workflow

1. Choose the closest profile or pilot template.
2. Fork the repository to your account or organization.
3. Rename and customize it with information you are allowed to publish.
4. Add services, projects and evidence using the included templates.
5. Use Zorgax for guided onboarding when useful.
6. Connect relevant offers or needs to the MyZubster Marketplace.
7. Contribute reusable improvements back through a pull request.

A repository, fork, demo or proposal is not by itself proof of employment, certification, partnership, funding, commercial availability or verified results. Keep credentials, private identity documents, wallet secrets and confidential information out of public repositories.
