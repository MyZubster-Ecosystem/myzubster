# Marketplace Demo Expansion — 13 Sep 2026

MyZubster's public Marketplace demo has been expanded to make the ecosystem easier to understand and test without requiring a real transaction.

Public demo: https://www.myzubster.com/marketplace-demos

## What is now visible

The demo includes clearly labeled examples across:

- coding, open source, DevOps, AI development and defensive cybersecurity;
- animals, responsible adoption, pet/community services and pollinator projects;
- sound systems, DJ/event equipment, subculture clothing and independent media exchange;
- repair, circular economy, local logistics, fermentation and water pilots;
- professional skills, volunteering, training and shared spaces;
- LIFE-oriented individual pilots, community/DAO pilots, MRV/evidence and Zorgax-assisted pilot flows.

The examples are intentionally demonstrative. They are not proof of real listings, adoption, partnerships, purchases or payments.

## Founding Seller trial

The Marketplace work also introduces a transparent 30-day Founding Seller trial and demo seller examples intended to show how seller participation could work. Trial/demo presentation must remain distinct from evidence of actual paid conversion.

## Contribution path

The next objective is not simply to add more categories. It is to convert public visitors into testers and contributors.

Beginner-friendly tasks:

- #1103 — verify Marketplace demos on mobile and desktop;
- #1104 — improve onboarding and contributor CTA;
- #1105 — add one new evidence-backed Marketplace demo category;
- #1106 — document a five-minute contributor quickstart.

Contributors should keep claims evidence-aware, avoid real personal data in demo content, and provide reproducible test evidence with pull requests.

## What would count as external growth

Useful external signals include new third-party pull requests, substantive issue reports, contributors, stars, forks used for actual development, or measurable traffic when that metric is available through an authorized source. Internal commit volume alone should not be presented as community growth.
