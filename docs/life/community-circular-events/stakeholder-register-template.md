# Stakeholder Register Template — Community Circular Events Pilot

**Status:** working template. Entries must be evidence-based and must not convert informal interest into a formal role.

## Purpose

Use this register to record potential and confirmed stakeholders for the candidate circular-events pilot while keeping clear separation between public interest, discussion, consent, participation and formal partnership.

## Allowed status values

- `DISCOVERED_PUBLICLY`
- `CONTACT_PLANNED`
- `CONTACTED`
- `INTEREST_EXPRESSED`
- `CONSENT_TO_DISCUSSION`
- `ROLE_UNDER_REVIEW`
- `PILOT_PARTICIPATION_PROPOSED`
- `PILOT_PARTICIPATION_CONFIRMED`
- `PARTNERSHIP_PROPOSED`
- `PARTNERSHIP_CONFIRMED`
- `DECLINED`
- `INACTIVE`

A social-media reaction, comment, follow or share must not be promoted beyond `DISCOVERED_PUBLICLY` or `INTEREST_EXPRESSED` unless stronger evidence exists.

## Evidence rules

For every promotion in status, record an evidence reference such as:

- public statement URL;
- email/message explicitly confirming interest or consent;
- signed or formally approved document;
- issue/PR comment attributable to the stakeholder;
- meeting note approved by the relevant party;
- formal letter, MoU or agreement where partnership is claimed.

Do not store passwords, tokens, private credentials, medical information or unnecessary personal data in this register.

## Consent model

Consent should be purpose-specific. Useful fields to track outside public output include:

- consent to contact;
- consent to be listed publicly;
- consent to participate in pilot design;
- consent to participate in field activities;
- consent to publication of non-sensitive results;
- consent to use name/logo, if relevant and separately granted.

Absence of objection is not consent.

## Promotion rules

`DISCOVERED_PUBLICLY -> CONTACTED` requires evidence that outreach occurred.

`CONTACTED -> INTEREST_EXPRESSED` requires a reply or attributable statement.

`INTEREST_EXPRESSED -> PILOT_PARTICIPATION_CONFIRMED` requires explicit confirmation of role and scope.

`PILOT_PARTICIPATION_CONFIRMED -> PARTNERSHIP_CONFIRMED` requires a separate formal basis; pilot participation alone is not partnership.

## Reporting rule

Public reporting should aggregate or anonymise stakeholder-interest metrics unless a stakeholder has explicitly agreed to be named.
