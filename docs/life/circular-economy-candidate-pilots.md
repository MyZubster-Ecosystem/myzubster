# MyZubster LIFE — Circular Economy Candidate Pilot Tracks

Status: **CANDIDATE / EXPLORATORY**  
Scope: internal MyZubster/Zorgax LIFE preparatory work.  

This document records candidate environmental pilot tracks that could use the existing MyZubster evidence, provenance, baseline and KPI framework. It does **not** assert EU LIFE funding, endorsement, consortium membership, formal partnership, confirmed pilot sites, validated environmental outcomes or participant consent.

## Shared MyZubster role

For each candidate track, MyZubster may be evaluated as a digital evidence layer connecting:

1. baseline definition;
2. material-flow or environmental events;
3. provenance and evidence records;
4. measurable indicators/KPIs;
5. reproducible reporting and audit trails.

Any real deployment requires an explicitly confirmed organisation/site, an agreed baseline and authorized data.

## Candidate track A — Absorbent Hygiene Products (AHP)

Examples include diapers/nappies, sanitary absorbent products and incontinence products.

Candidate circular-economy flow:

`collection → traceable batch → treatment → recovered material → destination/use → environmental evidence`

Potential measurements to validate with a real pilot include:

- mass collected and treated;
- recovery yield by material fraction;
- rejected/residual fraction;
- destination of recovered secondary materials;
- traceability/evidence completeness;
- environmental indicators defined against an agreed baseline.

Status: `CANDIDATE_PILOT_TRACK`.

No recycler, municipality, waste operator, technology provider or other organisation is represented here as a confirmed MyZubster or LIFE partner.

## Candidate track B — Industrial hemp circular bioeconomy

This track is intentionally framed around **industrial hemp and lawful agro-industrial biomass/residues**, not recreational cannabis or controlled-substance commerce.

Candidate circular-economy flow:

`cultivation/processing residue → traceable biomass/material lot → transformation/reuse → secondary material/product → evidence`

Potential measurements to validate with a real pilot include:

- biomass/residue input;
- material recovery or valorisation yield;
- waste avoided/diverted where demonstrable;
- destination/use of outputs;
- provenance/evidence completeness;
- environmental indicators defined against an agreed baseline.

Status: `CANDIDATE_PILOT_TRACK`.

No grower, processor, research body or industrial organisation is represented here as a confirmed MyZubster or LIFE partner.

## Evidence and governance gates

A candidate track can advance only when evidence supports the relevant gate:

- `CANDIDATE_PILOT_TRACK` — public concept only;
- `STAKEHOLDER_IN_DISCUSSION` — a real organisation is in a documented discussion, without inferred consent;
- `PILOT_SCOPE_IN_DEFINITION` — site/use case, data boundary and intended measurements are being explicitly defined;
- `PILOT_CONFIRMED` — requires explicit confirmation from the relevant organisation and a bounded pilot scope;
- environmental outcome claims require measured evidence and must not be inferred from participation.

Participant, contributor, candidate/stakeholder and formal consortium/partner evidence remain separate. A GitHub contribution, email conversation, meeting or candidate listing does not by itself create partnership, participant consent, funding, financial rights or EU LIFE status.

## Relationship to the existing framework

These tracks are intended to reuse the repository's baseline/KPI/evidence/provenance work rather than create a parallel evidence model. Pilot-specific schemas or integrations should only be added once a real use case and data boundary are confirmed.
