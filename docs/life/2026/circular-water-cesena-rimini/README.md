# LIFE 2026 — Circular Water Cesena–Rimini

## MyZubster dossier

Operational dossier supporting the development of a LIFE 2026 proposal focused on circular water, environmental MRV, interoperability, scientific validation and territorial replication in the Cesena–Rimini area.

### Scope

The dossier brings together material relevant to:

- circular water and water reuse pilots;
- environmental Monitoring, Reporting & Verification (MRV);
- IoT and operational-data interoperability;
- scientific validation and environmental indicators;
- public-authority / utility cooperation;
- Cesena–Rimini territorial demonstration;
- European replication and open-source deployment.

### Proposed MyZubster role

MyZubster is positioned as **Digital Infrastructure & Replication Technology Partner**, covering data ingestion, APIs, geospatial integration, evidence provenance, MRV workflows, dashboards, digital environmental records and replication tooling.

### Working partner architecture

Potential roles under discussion include public authorities / coordinator, water and wastewater infrastructure operators, scientific validation partners and European replication partners.

> Status: working material for consortium development. Roles, pilot perimeter, datasets, indicators, budget and formal participation remain subject to partner confirmation.
