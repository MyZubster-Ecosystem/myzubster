# MyZubster LIFE — Community pilot portfolio

## Status

**PREPARATORY / CONCEPT PORTFOLIO / NOT AUTHORISED / NOT AN OFFICIAL EU LIFE PROJECT LIST**

This directory translates the MyZubster community activity taxonomy into eight bounded pilot concepts. The documents are working briefs for GitHub scoping, stakeholder discovery, safety review and evidence design.

They do not imply LIFE funding, EU/CINEA endorsement, an approved application, a confirmed site, partner participation, budget availability or permission to execute real-world activities.

## Relationship to the environmental core

The environmental evidence chain remains central:

~~~text
ENVIRONMENTAL OR TERRITORIAL NEED
→ AUTHORISED ACTIVITY
→ MINIMISED EVIDENCE
→ VALIDATION
→ KPI / REPORT
→ REPLICATION DECISION
~~~

Music, sport, arts and other community tracks may support participation, communication, education, inclusion or data collection only when the connection to a defined objective is explicit and reviewable.

## Shared template

Use [PILOT_TEMPLATE.md](./PILOT_TEMPLATE.md) to turn a concept into a scoped issue or implementation document. The template requires roles, authorisations, baseline, KPI definitions, evidence classification, privacy/safety gates, risks, deliverables and a final decision.

## Portfolio gates

A concept may become **planned** only when:

1. the question and environmental/territorial objective are bounded;
2. a pilot owner explicitly opts in;
3. the candidate site and competent authority are identified;
4. data, privacy, safeguarding and safety constraints are reviewed;
5. baseline/KPI methodology is credible;
6. expected outputs and acceptance criteria are testable.

A planned activity may become **performed** only after the required authorisations. It may become a **verified contribution** only after evidence review. It may be called an **authorised pilot activity** only when the competent pilot authority has explicitly approved it.

## Selection criteria

Score 0–5, with evidence, for:

- environmental or territorial relevance;
- measurable and proportionate KPI;
- authorisation feasibility;
- safety and accessibility;
- privacy and data minimisation;
- contributor capacity and explicit opt-in;
- public deliverable value;
- replication potential.

Do not start all eight pilots at once. Select a small first cohort only after comparing scores, dependencies and reviewer capacity.

## Common public evidence

Each active pilot should expose:

- one canonical GitHub issue;
- current status and responsible roles;
- linked concept/template;
- public deliverables or controlled-storage references;
- baseline/KPI definitions;
- evidence and review state;
- risks, deviations and claim boundary;
- stop/revise/repeat/replicate decision.
