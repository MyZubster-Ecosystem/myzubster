# MyZubster Circular Care — Supply Validation v0.1

Venture: `MZ-VENTURE-AHP-001`  
State: `SUPPLY_VALIDATION`  
Effective date: 2026-09-02

This record starts documented supplier and treatment-route validation for MyZubster Circular Care. It records **publicly evidenced capabilities only**. No company below is represented as a confirmed supplier, partner, recycler, customer, pilot site or commercial counterparty.

## Evidence classes

- `PUBLIC_CAPABILITY_EVIDENCED` — an official/public source supports the stated production or treatment capability.
- `COMMERCIAL_TERMS_PENDING` — MOQ, price, lead time, payment terms, capacity allocation and contract terms are not evidenced yet.
- `PARTNERSHIP_NOT_CONFIRMED` — no commercial or pilot relationship with MyZubster is claimed.

## Candidate manufacturing routes

### 1. Fippi S.p.A. — Italy

Candidate scope: **baby diapers / nappies**.

Public evidence:

- Fippi states that it has operated in private-label personal-hygiene production for more than 50 years and supports private-label customers with product and packaging development: https://www.fippi.com/it/private-label/
- Fippi states that its disposable baby nappies are produced in Italy and offers baby-care products for private label: https://www.fippi.com/baby-care/

Validation status: `PUBLIC_CAPABILITY_EVIDENCED / COMMERCIAL_TERMS_PENDING / PARTNERSHIP_NOT_CONFIRMED`.

Required next evidence: MOQ by size/SKU, ex-works unit price, sample process, artwork/packaging minimums, lead time, production capacity, quality documentation, certifications, Incoterms and payment terms.

### 2. Drylock Technologies — Europe

Candidate scope: **baby care, feminine care and adult incontinence**.

Public evidence:

- Drylock explicitly offers retailer-brand/private-label feminine-care customization: https://drylocktechnologies.com/products/feminine-care/
- Drylock explicitly offers retailer-brand/private-label adult-care customization and a complete incontinence portfolio: https://drylocktechnologies.com/adult-care/

Validation status: `PUBLIC_CAPABILITY_EVIDENCED / COMMERCIAL_TERMS_PENDING / PARTNERSHIP_NOT_CONFIRMED`.

Required next evidence: exact EU production site for proposed SKUs, MOQ, unit price, formulation/material choices, packaging options, lead time, logistics, certifications, quality release process and commercial terms.

## Candidate circular-treatment route

### Contarina / Fater absorbent-hygiene-products recycling technology — Italy

Candidate scope: **post-consumer absorbent hygiene products (AHP)**.

Public evidence:

- Contarina's company profile describes an AHP recycling facility using Fater technology to sanitize and separate used diapers into secondary raw materials including plastics, cellulose and absorbent material, with stated authorized capacity up to 10,000 tonnes/year: https://contarina.it/files/en/Company_profile.pdf
- Contarina has historically described the Lovadina di Spresiano industrial facility as treating used baby diapers, incontinence products and sanitary absorbent products: https://contarina.it/eventi/12/25-ottobre-2017-inaugurato-a-treviso-il-primo-impianto-industriale-al-mondo-che-ricicla-il-100-dei-pannolini-usati

Validation status: `PUBLIC_TECHNICAL_CAPABILITY_EVIDENCED / COMMERCIAL_AVAILABILITY_PENDING / PARTNERSHIP_NOT_CONFIRMED`.

This evidence does **not** establish that Contarina or Fater is currently accepting MyZubster material, offering a commercial service to MyZubster, or willing to participate in a pilot. Those points require explicit confirmation.

## EU/Italy compliance discovery inputs

Before sale, the exact product configuration and claims require professional compliance review. Initial regulatory inputs to validate include:

1. **General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988** — applies from 13 December 2024 and provides the general EU safety framework for consumer products not fully covered by more specific rules: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj/eng
2. **REACH / substances in articles** — producers, importers and suppliers can have communication/notification obligations where Candidate List substances are present under applicable conditions: https://echa.europa.eu/regulations/reach/candidate-list-substances-in-articles
3. **EU Ecolabel for absorbent hygiene products** — Commission Decision (EU) 2023/1809 defines optional EU Ecolabel criteria for absorbent hygiene products, currently valid through 31 December 2029: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32023D1809

This section is a compliance-discovery checklist, not legal advice and not a statement that any proposed MyZubster product already complies.

## Supply-validation decision

The venture can advance from `VENTURE_DESIGN` to `SUPPLY_VALIDATION` because multiple plausible manufacturing routes and one circular-treatment route are now supported by public capability evidence and are under documented validation.

It **cannot** advance to `PILOT_SCOPE_IN_DEFINITION` merely from these public sources. Before that transition, MyZubster should record at minimum:

- one supplier response or quotation with usable MOQ/cost/lead-time data;
- a selected first product family/specification;
- a credible collection/treatment pilot candidate with explicit discussion or confirmation;
- initial product-compliance review boundaries;
- a first evidence-backed unit-economics scenario.
