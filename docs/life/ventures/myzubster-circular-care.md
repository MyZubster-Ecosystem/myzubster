# MyZubster Circular Care — Venture Charter v0.1

Status: **VENTURE_DESIGN / PRE-INCORPORATION**  
Venture ID: `MZ-VENTURE-AHP-001`  
Parent ecosystem: **MyZubster / Zorgax**  
Working name: **MyZubster Circular Care**  
Scope: absorbent hygiene products (AHP) — diapers/nappies, menstrual absorbent products and incontinence products.

> This record creates an operational venture inside the MyZubster project. It does **not** represent a legally incorporated company, registered trademark, manufacturing authorization, confirmed recycler, confirmed municipality, LIFE funding, partnership or environmental-performance claim.

## 1. Mission

Build a circular-care business that combines useful hygiene products with verifiable material-flow recovery.

The intended long-term loop is:

`product design/procurement → sale/use → collection → traceable batch → treatment → recovered material → destination/reuse → verified evidence → circularity report`

MyZubster provides the digital evidence, provenance, KPI and audit layer. Zorgax can support operational automation, reporting and controlled access to services.

## 2. Launch model

The default launch strategy is **asset-light first** rather than immediately building a factory.

### Phase A — Brand + supply

- create a MyZubster-owned circular-care product line using qualified third-party manufacturing/private-label supply;
- define product specifications, packaging, traceability identifiers and supplier evidence requirements;
- keep manufacturing claims limited to evidence actually supplied by the producer;
- complete legal, safety, labeling and market-access review before any commercial launch.

### Phase B — Collection pilot

- start a bounded AHP collection pilot with an explicitly confirmed site/operator;
- assign traceable batch/lot identifiers;
- record mass collected, rejected fraction, treatment destination and evidence references;
- do not claim recycling or environmental benefit until treatment and outcomes are independently evidenced.

### Phase C — Circular recovery

- connect a qualified treatment/recycling partner;
- trace recovered material fractions and their destination/use;
- calculate predefined circularity KPIs against an agreed baseline;
- publish only verified results.

### Phase D — Scale

- expand to additional sites, B2B customers and territories only after the pilot evidence model is reproducible;
- evaluate owned manufacturing or owned treatment capacity only when volumes and economics justify the capital requirement.

## 3. Product and service lines

Initial candidate product families:

1. baby diapers/nappies;
2. menstrual absorbent products;
3. adult incontinence products.

Candidate revenue lines:

- product margin from branded/private-label AHP;
- B2B supply subscriptions for care facilities, hospitality or other qualified customers;
- collection/service contracts;
- traceability, compliance-evidence and circularity reporting services;
- premium MyZubster/Zorgax operational tooling for participating organisations.

No revenue is considered realized until supported by actual orders, contracts or verified payment records.

## 4. MyZubster system role

Each real pilot should be represented as a bounded evidence object with:

- venture and pilot identifiers;
- organisation/site confirmation state;
- data authorization reference;
- baseline reference;
- product/batch identifiers where applicable;
- material-flow event references;
- predefined KPI definitions;
- treatment/recovery evidence;
- destination/use evidence;
- report reference;
- human verification state.

The existing `CANDIDATE_PILOT_TRACK` governance remains authoritative until the required evidence gates are satisfied.

## 5. Core KPIs

Pilot KPIs must be defined before evaluating outcomes. Candidate metrics include:

- units supplied;
- kilograms collected;
- collection rate where a valid denominator exists;
- kilograms treated;
- recovery yield by material fraction;
- rejected/residual fraction;
- kilograms of verified secondary material produced;
- verified destination/use rate;
- evidence completeness rate;
- cost per kilogram collected/treated;
- gross margin by product/service line;
- contribution margin per participating site.

Environmental impact metrics require a documented baseline, method, units and evidence source before publication.

## 6. Governance states

The venture may progress through these internal states:

- `VENTURE_DESIGN` — business and evidence model defined;
- `SUPPLY_VALIDATION` — candidate products/suppliers under documented validation;
- `PILOT_SCOPE_IN_DEFINITION` — site, operator, data boundary and measurements being defined;
- `PILOT_CONFIRMED` — explicit organisation/site confirmation and bounded scope recorded;
- `COMMERCIAL_PILOT` — real commercial activity with verified orders/contracts and pilot evidence;
- `OPERATING_BUSINESS` — repeatable sales and operations demonstrated;
- `LEGAL_ENTITY_CONFIRMED` — separate legal incorporation evidence recorded.

A later state must never be inferred from GitHub activity alone.

## 7. First execution gates

To move from this charter to a real company launch, MyZubster must obtain and record:

1. a proposed jurisdiction and legal structure reviewed by a qualified professional;
2. a cleared business/brand name before external commercial use;
3. at least one validated product/supplier route or documented decision to manufacture directly;
4. cost, MOQ, lead-time and quality documentation sufficient for a unit-economics model;
5. a confirmed collection/treatment pilot partner or site;
6. authorized pilot data boundaries;
7. baseline + KPI definitions;
8. product/legal/safety/labeling review appropriate to the intended markets;
9. verified commercial evidence before any revenue claim.

## 8. Initial strategic decision

**Default recommendation recorded by the venture:** launch first as a MyZubster-controlled brand and circular-service operator using third-party qualified production and treatment partners. This minimizes early capital expenditure while allowing MyZubster to prove demand, unit economics and circular-flow evidence before evaluating owned factories or recycling plants.

## 9. Relationship to LIFE / external programs

This venture may reuse MyZubster LIFE-oriented evidence and KPI work, but it must remain separate from any claim of LIFE funding, endorsement, consortium membership or formal partnership unless explicit external evidence supports that claim.
