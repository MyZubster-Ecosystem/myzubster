# Daniel Ioni — Kefir, volunteering and the roots of MyZubster

Status: **FOUNDER'S PERSONAL TESTIMONY / NOT INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED**

Daniel Ioni states that he learned about kefir while volunteering in community settings associated, in his account, with the **Comunità Papa Giovanni**.

He recalls a simple question arising during everyday community life: if yogurt-like fermented foods were bought repeatedly for breakfast, could part of that food be produced locally instead, with appropriate care, knowledge and responsibility?

Daniel says that, on his own initiative, he began cultivating kefir grains obtained through direct person-to-person exchange. He then experimented in domestic and community kitchens with milk fermentation and with foods such as fresh cheese, ricotta-style preparations and sweet or savoury recipes. In his account, this expanded the kitchen's capacity for self-production and reduced reliance on repeatedly purchased products.

He describes these activities as personal, small-scale experiments developed during his volunteer experience—not as an official programme, clinical intervention, approved food business or initiative formally adopted by the Comunità Papa Giovanni or any therapeutic facility. No claim is made here about authorization, quantified savings, nutritional superiority, medical benefit or suitability for vulnerable people.

Daniel says that he still maintains kefir cultures at his home and at his grandmother's home. This continuity influenced a central MyZubster idea: useful knowledge can travel through communities like a living culture—shared, cared for, reproduced and documented—while responsibility, consent and safety remain with real people.

## Influence on MyZubster

The experience contributes to four design principles:

1. **Regeneration instead of repeated consumption** — where lawful and safe, maintain a reusable capability rather than buying the same input again.
2. **Peer-to-peer knowledge** — practical knowledge and starter cultures can move through trusted human exchange.
3. **Local circularity** — reusable containers, controlled batches and responsible use of surplus can be measured.
4. **Evidence before claims** — personal experience inspires a pilot, but does not prove safety, savings or environmental impact.

These principles now inform the candidate [MyZubster Kefir Circular Pilot](KEFIR_CIRCULAR_PILOT.md), which proposes a synthetic-first evidence system for culture lineage, batches, quality checks, reusable containers and minimal blockchain anchoring.
