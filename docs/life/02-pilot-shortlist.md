# LIFE Pilot Shortlist — v0.1

## Purpose

Select a protected-area pilot candidate for a LIFE 2026 concept note without presenting any organisation as a partner before formal confirmation.

## Candidate A — Isola di Capraia / Tuscan Archipelago context

**Why consider it:** strong fit with biodiversity, protected-area monitoring, territorial observation and a compact pilot footprint.

**To verify before outreach:** competent authority, existing monitoring programmes, available datasets, access permissions, operational constraints, baseline indicators and willingness to participate.

**Proposed pilot:** structured field observations → evidence registry → validation workflow → institutional dashboard → LIFE KPI reporting.

## Candidate B — Parco Nazionale delle Cinque Terre

**Why consider it:** protected-area setting with strong visitor/territory interface and potential for biodiversity, monitoring and citizen-observation use cases.

**To verify before outreach:** competent authority, current digital/monitoring initiatives, data availability, interoperability constraints, eligible activities and pilot contact.

## Candidate C — Parco Nazionale dell'Arcipelago Toscano

**Why consider it:** direct relevance to island biodiversity and territorial monitoring, with potential for a replicable Mediterranean protected-area model.

**To verify before outreach:** competent authority, existing biodiversity monitoring, datasets/APIs, field access, baseline and candidate pilot site.

## Selection criteria

Score each candidate 0–5 on:

1. biodiversity/nature relevance;
2. measurable baseline and KPI availability;
3. institutional readiness;
4. data/interoperability feasibility;
5. feasibility within LIFE 2026 timing;
6. replication potential.

**Important:** this is a working shortlist, not evidence that any listed organisation has agreed to participate.
