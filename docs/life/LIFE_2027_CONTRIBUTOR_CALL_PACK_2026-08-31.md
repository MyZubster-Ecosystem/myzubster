## Status and scope

This is a preparatory MyZubster LIFE 2027 working pack for contributors and invited stakeholders. It does not claim EU LIFE funding, endorsement, formal consortium membership, partner status, budget allocation or confirmed pilot ownership.

## Working objective for the call

Turn the current evidence-first proposal into a small set of concrete decisions:

1. confirm or correct the priority pilot;
2. select 3–5 KPI/MRV indicators for the first end-to-end test;
3. identify the operational pilot owner and data owner;
4. confirm or correct the proposed responsibility matrix;
5. agree the first deliverable and next formalisation gate.

## Pilot proposal — for discussion, not confirmation

### Primary candidate — Water & Agriculture

Working rationale: it combines water management, agronomic data, environmental impact and validation in a measurable case.

Minimum test path:

`authorised or synthetic data → provenance → quality checks → baseline → KPI/MRV calculation → evidence record → human validation`

### Secondary candidate — Circular Water & Reuse

Activate when an operational site, legitimate dataset access and pilot owner are confirmed.

### Cross-cutting layer — Territorial Replication & Digital MRV

Use the replication package as a method across pilots rather than as the first standalone pilot.

## Initial KPI/MRV shortlist

Candidate core indicators for the first test:

- reused water volume / reuse share, when applicable;
- avoided primary water withdrawal against a comparable baseline;
- dataset completeness;
- percentage of records with verifiable provenance;
- reproducibility of KPI calculation from documented sources, formula and version.

Optional case-specific indicators may include water quality, water/agronomic efficiency, recovered nutrients/materials and energy per unit of service.

No target should be presented as validated before a comparable baseline, source data and validation method are agreed.

## Proposed responsibility matrix — to validate

- **MyZubster** — candidate owner of digital evidence, provenance, data integration and MRV orchestration.
- **Maggioli** — possible contribution to project design/management, digitalisation and territorial replication; scope to be defined.

- **CRPA / other technical actors** — possible field monitoring, validation or gap-filling role only after explicit confirmation.
- **GitHub LIFE contributors** — may contribute to bounded technical/evidence tasks without this implying consortium or partner status.
