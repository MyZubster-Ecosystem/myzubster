## English

### 1. Introduction

MyZubster ("we", "our", "the Platform") operates a plant, pet, and smart garden ecosystem platform ("Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal data when you use our Services, including but not limited to:

- NFC pet tags and Arduino smart garden sensors;
- Monero (XMR) cryptocurrency payments and escrow transactions;
- Global Plant Map, Seed Exchange P2P, and Tari blockchain NFT contribution certificates;
- AI-powered plant and pet analysis, community verification, and voting mechanisms.

We are committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Italian privacy laws (D.Lgs. 196/2003 and D.Lgs. 101/2018).

By using our Services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our Services.

### 2. Data Collected

We collect and process the following categories of personal data:

| Category | Specific Data | Purpose |
|----------|---------------|---------|
| **Identity Data** | Username, email address, wallet addresses (XMR), Tari NFT wallet addresses | Account creation, authentication, and payments |
| **IoT & Sensor Data** | NFC pet tag IDs, Arduino sensor readings (soil moisture, temperature, humidity, light), timestamps, geolocation (when enabled) | Smart garden monitoring and pet tracking features |
| **Content Data** | Photos of plants/pets, map markers, seed exchange listings, community votes, AI-generated analysis | Service functionality and community features |
| **Technical Data** | IP address, browser type, device identifiers, API logs, session tokens | Security, analytics, and service optimization |
| **Payment Data** | Monero transaction hashes, escrow status, payout addresses | Payment processing and dispute resolution |
| **Communication Data** | Support tickets, community messages, feedback | Customer support and service improvement |

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

### 3. Purposes

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

1. **Service Delivery**: Providing access to the MyZubster platform, IoT device integration (NFC, Arduino), and blockchain-based features (XMR payments, Tari NFTs);
2. **Account Management**: Creating and managing user accounts, wallet associations, and profile settings;
3. **Payment Processing**: Facilitating Monero payments, managing escrow transactions, and ensuring secure settlements;
4. **AI & Community Verification**: Running AI analysis on plant and pet data, processing community votes, and maintaining the integrity of the Global Plant Map;
5. **Seed Exchange**: Enabling P2P seed exchange listings, matching, and community ratings;
6. **Communications**: Sending service-related notifications, security alerts, and updates;
7. **Security & Fraud Prevention**: Detecting unauthorized transactions, preventing abuse, and protecting platform integrity;
8. **Legal Compliance**: Meeting regulatory obligations, responding to legal requests, and enforcing our Terms of Service;
9. **Improvements**: Analyzing usage patterns to improve features, performance, and user experience.

### 6. User Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

- **Right of Access**: Request copies of your personal data;
- **Right to Rectification**: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **Right to Erasure**: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten");
- **Right to Restrict Processing**: Request limitation of processing in certain circumstances;
- **Right to Data Portability**: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format;
- **Right to Object**: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing;
- **Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making**: Request human intervention or challenge automated decisions (including AI analysis of plants/pets).

**To exercise these rights**, contact our DPO using the details in Section 10. We will respond within 30 days of receiving a verified request.

**Withdrawal of Consent**: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

**Right to Complain**: You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or your local supervisory authority.

### 8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data:

- **Encryption**: End-to-end encryption for sensitive data (payment details, wallet addresses), TLS 1.3 for data in transit;
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, and principle of least privilege;
- **Blockchain Security**: Monero transactions use ring signatures and stealth addresses; Tari NFT data is immutably stored on-chain;
- **Infrastructure**: Italian VPS deployment with firewall protection, DDoS mitigation, and regular security audits;
- **IoT Security**: NFC tags use unique identifiers with optional encryption; Arduino devices communicate over secured channels;
- **Incident Response**: Established procedures for security incidents, including 72-hour notification to supervisory authorities where required;
- **Data Minimization**: We only collect and retain data necessary for the specified purposes.

Despite our efforts, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but continuously improve our measures.

### 9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy:

| Data Category | Retention Period | Legal Basis |

| **Payment & Escrow Data** | 7 years (Italian tax and anti-money laundering requirements) | Legal Obligation |
| **IoT & Sensor Data** | Until deletion request or 3 years (whichever is earlier) | Consent / Legitimate Interest |
| **Blockchain Records** | Indefinitely (immutable public ledger) | Technical necessity |
| **AI Analysis & Community Votes** | Until account deletion + 1 year (for dispute resolution) | Legitimate Interest |
| **Communication & Support** | 2 years from last interaction | Legitimate Interest / Legal Obligation |

Upon account deletion, we will anonymize or delete personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., financial records, tax obligations).

### 8. Sicurezza

Implementiamo misure tecniche e organizzative appropriate per proteggere i tuoi dati personali:

- **Crittografia**: Crittografia end-to-end per dati sensibili (dettagli pagamenti, indirizzi wallet), TLS 1.3 per dati in transito;
- **Controlli di Accesso**: Controlli di accesso basati su ruoli, autenticazione a più fattori per l'accesso amministrativo e principio del minimo privilegio;
- **Sicurezza Blockchain**: Le transazioni Monero utilizzano firme ad anello e indirizzi stealth; i dati NFT Tari sono immutabilmente archiviati on-chain;
- **Infrastruttura**: Distribuzione su VPS italiano con protezione firewall, mitigazione DDoS e audit di sicurezza regolari;
- **Sicurezza IoT**: I tag NFC utilizzano identificatori univoci con crittografia opzionale; i dispositivi Arduino comunicano su canali protetti;
- **Risposta agli Incidenti**: Procedure stabilite per incidenti di sicurezza, inclusa notifica entro 72 ore alle autorità di vigilanza ove richiesto;
- **Minimizzazione dei Dati**: Raccogliamo e conserviamo solo i dati necessari per le finalità specificate.

Nonostante i nostri sforzi, nessun metodo di trasmissione o archiviazione è sicuro al 100%. Non possiamo garantire la sicurezza assoluta ma miglioriamo continuamente le nostre misure.
