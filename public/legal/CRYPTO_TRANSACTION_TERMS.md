## English Version

### 1. Orders

1.1. All orders placed on the MyZubster platform for physical goods (seeds, NFC pet tags, Arduino sensor kits), digital services (global plant map access, Seed Exchange P2P matching), and NFT certificates (Tari blockchain contributions) are subject to these Terms.

1.2. Prices are displayed in EUR or XMR at the user's election. XMR prices are converted from EUR reference prices at the spot rate at the moment of order confirmation.

1.3. Orders are confirmed only upon (a) receipt of the applicable XMR amount in the platform's escrow wallet, or (b) explicit bank-transfer confirmation for fiat settlements.

1.4. MyZubster reserves the right to cancel orders involving regulated agricultural products (seeds, plants) that violate EU phytosanitary Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 or Italian Legislative Decree 214/2005.

1.5. For orders involving cross-border seed shipment, the Buyer is responsible for import permits, phytosanitary certificates, and any customs duties under EU and destination-country law.

### 2. Escrow

2.1. All XMR transactions on MyZubster are processed through a non-custodial escrow smart contract. Funds are locked at the time of order placement and released only upon mutual confirmation or dispute resolution.

2.2. **Escrow Release Triggers:**
- Automatic release upon Buyer's delivery confirmation (tracking scan, NFC tag activation, or sensor data upload timestamp);
- Automatic release after 14 calendar days if no dispute is raised;
- Manual release by MyZubster arbitration panel per Section 3.

2.3. The escrow contract does not earn interest for MyZubster. XMR held in escrow is not commingled with platform operating funds.

2.4. In the event of an XMR network fork or consensus change, the parties agree to be bound by the chain recognized as valid by the majority of hashrate, without liability to MyZubster for chain-splitting losses.

### 3. Disputes

3.1. Disputes must be filed via the MyZubster in-app dispute form within 14 calendar days of the expected delivery date or service completion.

3.2. MyZubster operates a decentralized arbitration model:
- First tier: AI analysis of provided evidence (sensor data, NFC logs, map contribution hashes, blockchain transaction proofs);
- Second tier: Community jury of 7 randomly selected, reputation-scored users (minimum 3 successful prior trades);
- Third tier: Binding decision by a MyZubster-appointed independent arbiter under Italian law.

3.3. The arbitration process is conducted in English; parties may submit evidence in any EU official language with certified translation at their own expense.

3.4. MyZubster acts solely as a technical escrow provider and arbitration facilitator. It is not a party to the underlying sale and bears no liability for the quality, legality, or safety of goods or data transacted.

### 4. Refunds

4.1. Refunds are processed from the escrow wallet to the Buyer's original XMR address. Fiat refunds (where applicable) are remitted via SEPA within 14 business days.

4.2. **Refund Eligibility:**
- Full refund: item not received, counterfeit goods, defective NFC/Arduino hardware verified by AI diagnostic logs;
- Partial refund: minor variance in plant species, incomplete NFT metadata, partial data-set delivery;
- No refund: Buyer's change of mind after 14-day withdrawal period, Buyer's failure to provide a receiving XMR address within 30 days, force-majeure delivery failure attributable to the Buyer's jurisdiction.

4.3. Refund claims are subject to a 30 EUR (or equivalent XMR) administrative fee for manual processing, except where the fault is verified as MyZubster's or the Seller's.

4.4. For Seed Exchange P2P transactions, refunds are limited to the escrowed service fee; biological products are non-returnable once shipped, per EU consumer-goods directives.

### 5. Volatility Disclaimer

5.1. XMR and other cryptocurrencies are speculative assets subject to extreme price volatility. MyZubster does not guarantee the fiat value of XMR held in escrow at the time of release.

5.2. Users acknowledge that:
- XMR transaction fees (monero_ringct) vary independently of MyZubster's control;
- Blockchain confirmation times may delay escrow release beyond estimated windows;
- Network congestion, mempool dynamics, or regulatory changes may affect transaction finality.

5.3. MyZubster is not a licensed payment-service provider under PSD2 (EU Directive 2015/2366). XMR escrow is provided as a technical service under Italian Civil Code Articles 1766–1786 (mandate and deposit).

### 6. EU / Italian Regulations

6.1. **GDPR Compliance.** MyZubster acts as data controller for all personal data processed through the platform, including NFC pet-tag identifiers, Arduino sensor location data, plant-map geolocation, and user-submitted biometric data for AI verification. Legal bases are:
- Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for transactional data;
- Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) for fraud prevention and community reputation scoring;
- Explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for biometric processing and NFT public-verification hashes.

6.2. **Data Subject Rights.** Users may exercise access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection rights via privacy@myzubster.eu or the in-app privacy dashboard. Requests are handled within 30 days per Art. 12 GDPR.

6.3. **Data Protection Officer (DPO).** MyZubster has appointed a DPO reachable at dpo@myzubster.eu for all GDPR and Italian D.Lgs. 196/2003 (as amended by D.Lgs. 101/2018) inquiries.

6.4. **Italian Specifics.** The platform is operated from an Italian VPS. MyZubster complies with:
- Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) guidelines on geolocation and IoT data (Provv. 8/2014, 46/2019);
- Italian Electronic Signature and Timestamping regulations (D.Lgs. 179/2016, eIDAS Regulation (EU) 910/2014) for NFT certificates;

6.5. **AML / CFT.** Pursuant to EU Directive 2015/849 and Italian D.Lgs. 231/2007, MyZubster conducts risk-based customer due diligence on users transacting above EUR 1,000 in cumulative XMR value per calendar year. Suspicious transaction reports are filed with the Italian Financial Intelligence Unit (UIF).

6.6. **Cross-Border Transfers.** No personal data is transferred outside the EU/EEA. Blockchain transaction hashes stored on Tari are pseudonymized metadata and do not constitute personal data transfers under Chapter V GDPR.

6.7. **Consumer Rights.** EU consumers enjoy a 14-day withdrawal right under Consumer Rights Directive (EU) 2011/83. This right is waived explicitly for NFT certificates and digital map-access services delivered instantaneously upon payment confirmation, per Art. 16(c) of the Directive.

### 7. Tax Declarations

7.1. **Seller Tax Obligations.** Sellers on MyZubster are independent contractors and are solely responsible for declaring and paying income tax, VAT, and social contributions in their jurisdiction of tax residence. MyZubster issues annual revenue summaries (Form T24) to Sellers with EU P2P transaction volume exceeding EUR 600 per calendar year.

7.2. **Buyer VAT.** For B2C transactions within the EU, VAT is collected at the Seller's EU member-state rate or, for intra-EU B2B sales, under the reverse-charge mechanism with valid VAT-ID validation.

7.3. **Italian Withholding.** For Italian-resident Sellers, MyZubster may withhold a 23% ritenuta alla fonte on gross payments exceeding EUR 5,000 per fiscal year, per Italian Income Tax Code (TUIR) Articles 25-28, unless a declaration of non-residence or VAT exemption is provided.

7.4. **Cryptocurrency Reporting.** Users are solely responsible for declaring XMR holdings, capital gains, and crypto-to-crypto conversions to their national tax authorities. MyZubster does not issue tax advice and recommends consultation with a qualified EU tax advisor.

7.5. **NFT Certificates.** Tari-based NFT contribution certificates may constitute taxable events under Italian Royal Decree 917/1986 (TUIR) and EU VAT rules for electronic services. Users should obtain independent tax counsel regarding the characterization of NFT receipts.

7.6. **Invoices.** MyZubster issues electronic invoices (Fattura Elettronica) for Italian transactions and standard EU invoices for cross-border B2B transactions, compliant with Italian D.Lgs. 127/2015 and EU Directive 2014/55.
