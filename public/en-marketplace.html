MyZubster Marketplace

The Marketplace connects community members who want to offer or request skills, services, products and project support. Categories include development, events and sound systems, agriculture, arts, wellbeing, pets, university courses, thesis projects and research.

How it works

Create a profile, prepare a listing, choose a category and exchange mode, review the draft, and publish only when you decide to. Zorgax can help fill the draft but does not automatically publish it.

Exchange modes

Listings may use barter, free/gift modes or supported payment references such as MYZ, XMR and other rails where available. Availability of a label in a listing does not by itself imply automated on-chain settlement.

Community use cases

Developers can support local projects, event crews can offer technical services, farmers and seed communities can exchange knowledge and resources, pet communities can coordinate responsible services, and students or researchers can publish academic opportunities.
