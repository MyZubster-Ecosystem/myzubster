Payment Dashboard

Funding flow exposed as separate, auditable layers. Unknown values are shown as Unknown — never as zero.

Connection

Endpoints require a Bearer JWT. Treasury layers (funding inputs, available balance, conversion) additionally require an admin role.

Source warnings

Balances

MYZ is an internal reward accounting unit, not an on-chain balance. XMR treasury balance lives in the Monero wallet RPC and is reported as unknown when it is not configured.

Funding flow

States are independent. Receiving BTC or a Stripe payment never approves a bounty.

Filters

1 · Incoming funding

BTC receipts and Stripe settlements. Funding inputs only — they are not bounty approvals.

2 · Available balance

Counts settled funding inputs only. Unconfirmed and failed inputs are excluded.

3 · Conversion

BTC/fiat → XMR conversion is a separate backend integration. Stripe does not convert to XMR.

4 · Escrow

Escrow is tracked separately from payout status.

5 · Bounty rewards (MYZ ledger)

Internal MYZ accounting entries. A bounty moves to payable only after review and approval.

6 · XMR payouts

External settlement. Idempotency keys and retry posture are shown per item.
