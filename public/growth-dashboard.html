How to read growth

More visibility can create more opportunities for participation; participation can eventually produce stronger adoption evidence. They are not interchangeable.

1. Visibility

External articles, social mentions and discoverability signals. Useful for reach, but not proof that people are using the product.

— signals

2. Participation

Public GitHub activity, contributor aliases and merged external contributions. Stronger than passive attention because it produces inspectable artifacts.

— signals

3. Adoption

Independent integrations, deployments and repeated external use. These require a higher evidence bar and are tracked separately.

— verified
