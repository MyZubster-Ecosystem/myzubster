30-second description

MyZubster explores how real-world observations and authorized datasets can move through provenance, validation and human review into reusable evidence. The ecosystem combines open-source development, community contribution, mapping, bounties, automation through the Zorgax track, governance experiments and controlled pilot architecture.

Status boundary: MyZubster is in active development and validation. Experimental features, roadmap items, discussions and pre-candidature work must not be reported as completed deployments, formal partnerships, adoption, funding or institutional endorsement.

Environmental / LIFE 2027 direction

MyZubster is preparing an environmental pilot architecture toward a possible LIFE 2027 pathway, including data provenance, KPI/evidence workflows, Zorgax-assisted automation and human scientific/technical review.

This is preparatory work. It does not imply an approved LIFE proposal, EU funding, European Commission/CINEA endorsement or a completed consortium.

Verification checklist for journalists

Before reporting a milestone, verify the repository/version, the public or sanitized evidence, who independently performed the activity, whether it can be reproduced, and whether a stated partnership, pilot, payment or funding event has actually been formalized.
