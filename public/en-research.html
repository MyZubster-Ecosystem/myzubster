MyZubster Research

MyZubster can be used as an applied research environment where students, researchers and contributors study real open-source workflows while keeping academic validation and institutional responsibilities separate from community governance.

Bitcoin and Monero marketplace research

Compare transparent-ledger and privacy-oriented payment systems in a controlled Marketplace scenario, including transaction visibility, fungibility, metadata, user experience and compliance questions. The research pilot does not require real funds or private keys.

Open the BTC/XMR research pilot →

University-linked DAO governance

Study whether a separate DAO or community treasury can coordinate selected project budgets, grants and contributor rewards while preserving university independence, academic authority and legal accountability.

Open-source thesis pathways

Possible disciplines include computer science, cybersecurity, economics, law, governance, HCI, open science and environmental data systems. Repositories, issues and pull requests provide traceable project evidence, but do not automatically confer academic credits or institutional endorsement.
