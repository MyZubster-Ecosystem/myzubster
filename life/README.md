# LIFE MyZubster 2026

Working repository for the LIFE 2026 MyZubster proposal.

## Objective

Develop and demonstrate a circular-water system combining real-world water reuse, environmental monitoring, MRV and a Digital Environmental Passport.

## Pilot area

Initial territorial focus: Cesena–Rimini / Emilia-Romagna, Italy.

## Repository structure

- concept-note/ — Concept Note and proposal snapshots
- consortium/ — partners, roles and letters
- work-packages/ — WP descriptions, tasks and deliverables
- budget/ — preliminary and final budget
- kpi/ — environmental and project KPIs
- letters-of-intent/ — partner support documents
- evidence/ — technical and territorial evidence
