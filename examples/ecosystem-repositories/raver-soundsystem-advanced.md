# Example Repository — Raver / Sound System Crew (Advanced)

## Who we are
A demonstration profile for sound-system crews, free-party collectives, DJs, promoters, visual artists and underground cultural groups entering MyZubster.

## What we can offer
- sound system and event production
- DJ and live performance support
- lighting, visuals and stage setup
- equipment rental and technical assistance
- independent cultural events and community projects
- mentoring around DIY production and open tools

## What we need from the ecosystem
- developer help for websites, ticketing alternatives and community tools
- legal/safety guidance appropriate to local rules
- university research collaboration on subcultures and cultural economies
- marketplace discovery
- transport, repair, fabrication and power/technical support

## Marketplace examples
- PA and sound-system rental
- DJ equipment
- lights and visual equipment
- technician booking
- clothing, vinyl and subculture items
- event-production services

## Zorgax role
Zorgax can help onboard crews, structure technical riders, document equipment, connect collaborators and turn community needs into project briefs.

## Public evidence
Use public event documentation and equipment descriptions. Do not publish private locations for unannounced events, personal data, access credentials or information that could create safety/security risks.
