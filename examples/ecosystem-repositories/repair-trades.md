# Example Repository — Repair & Trades

## Who we are
A demonstration profile for electricians, plumbers, mechanics, repair technicians and skilled trades entering MyZubster.

## What we can offer
- repair and maintenance services
- installation and troubleshooting
- emergency or scheduled interventions
- tool and equipment knowledge
- practical mentoring and workshops

## What we need from the ecosystem
- booking and lead generation
- technical profile support
- clear service-area presentation without exposing private addresses
- documentation and customer communication tools
- collaboration with makers and developers

## Marketplace examples
- plumbing repair
- electrical troubleshooting
- appliance repair
- bicycle or equipment maintenance
- repair workshops

## Zorgax role
Zorgax can help triage requests, structure job descriptions, prepare estimates as drafts and route users to the appropriate specialist.

## Public evidence
Use certifications, portfolio photos with consent and project descriptions. Do not publish private customer addresses, access codes, invoices or sensitive property information.
