# Example Repository — Water / Circular Water

## Who we are
A demonstration profile for water utilities, researchers, municipalities, SMEs or communities working on circular water and resource efficiency.

## What we can offer
- pilot sites and operational context
- water reuse and efficiency use cases
- field measurements and observations
- technical collaboration opportunities
- environmental evidence for validation

## What we need
- researchers and universities
- IoT and developer support
- MRV/data methodology
- privacy-safe public evidence
- potential LIFE pilot collaboration

## Marketplace examples
- water audit support
- sensor installation assistance
- data analysis
- maintenance and monitoring services
- training/workshops

## Zorgax role
Zorgax can help organize requirements, evidence, tasks, stakeholders and pilot reporting.

## Privacy and evidence
Do not publish exact private infrastructure coordinates, credentials or sensitive operational details. Separate public evidence from restricted technical information.
