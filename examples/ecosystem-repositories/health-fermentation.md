# Example Repository — Health, Fermentation & Food Culture

## Who we are
A demonstration profile for fermentation communities, kefir makers, food-culture educators and non-clinical wellness initiatives entering MyZubster.

## What we can offer
- fermentation workshops
- kefir and food-culture knowledge sharing
- community education
- starter-culture exchange where lawful and appropriate
- documentation of traditional and circular food practices

## What we need from the ecosystem
- research collaboration
- documentation and evidence workflows
- event spaces and local partners
- digital tools and marketplace support
- university and laboratory connections for validated research

## Marketplace examples
- fermentation workshops
- educational sessions
- non-medical food-culture kits or materials
- community events
- research collaboration requests

## Zorgax role
Zorgax can help structure educational content, project briefs, pilot documentation and community onboarding. It must not present unverified health claims as medical advice.

## Public evidence
Use workshop documentation, public research links and project outputs. Do not publish personal health records, diagnoses or unsupported therapeutic claims.
