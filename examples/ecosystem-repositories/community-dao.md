# Example Repository — Community DAO

## Who we are
A demonstration profile for a community, collective or multi-stakeholder group that wants to coordinate shared projects in the MyZubster ecosystem.

## What the DAO can coordinate
- shared community priorities
- proposals and voting records
- pilot selection
- shared resources
- contributor roles
- transparent project evidence
- collaboration across Marketplace categories

## Example participants
- developers
- students and researchers
- sound system crews
- artists and makers
- farmers and circular economy participants
- local services and volunteers

## Suggested repository structure
- `README.md` — mission and membership model
- `proposals/` — public proposals
- `decisions/` — transparent decision records
- `projects/` — active pilots and collaborations
- `evidence/` — public proof of completed work

## Zorgax role
Zorgax can help summarize proposals, identify missing information, draft tasks, map contributors to projects and prepare transparent public updates.

## Governance principles
Participation should be voluntary, transparent and evidence-based. Repository records should not be presented as proof of legal incorporation, funding or external institutional approval unless independently verified.
