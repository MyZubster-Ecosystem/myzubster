# Example Repository — Animal Rescue & Pet Services

## Who we are
A demonstration profile for shelters, rescue volunteers, pet sitters, foster networks and animal-service communities entering MyZubster.

## What we can offer
- lost/found animal support
- adoption and fostering coordination
- pet sitting and walking
- transport and volunteer help
- education and community awareness

## What we need from the ecosystem
- verified local contacts
- developer support for forms and alerts
- volunteer coordination
- safe marketplace listings
- collaboration with local communities and universities

## Marketplace examples
- pet sitting
- dog walking
- animal transport assistance
- volunteer calls
- adoption-event support

## Zorgax role
Zorgax can help structure reports, volunteer requests and service listings, while avoiding unsupported veterinary or medical claims.

## Public evidence
Use animal photos only with appropriate context and consent from responsible organizations. Avoid publishing private home addresses, owner contact details without consent, or exact locations that could put animals at risk.
