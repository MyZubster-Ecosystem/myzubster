# Choose Your MyZubster Profile

Pick the template closest to who you are, fork it on GitHub, and turn it into your own independent profile, project, community or pilot repository.


## Interactive onboarding — from GitHub to MyZubster

Use the same public onboarding from every profile template:

[**Create / connect MyZubster with GitHub**](https://www.myzubster.com/social-login?returnTo=%2Fzorgax-profile-onboarding.html%3Fprovider%3Dgithub) · [**Create GitHub account**](https://github.com/signup) · [**Complete profile with Zorgax**](https://www.myzubster.com/zorgax-profile-onboarding.html) · [**Try Marketplace demos**](https://www.myzubster.com/marketplace-demos) · [**Open Marketplace**](https://www.myzubster.com/community-marketplace.html)

Choose a community below, open its repository, fork it, then use Zorgax to turn the fork into your profile/project and connect it to the Marketplace or a pilot.

## How it works

1. Choose one of the templates below.
2. Open the repository and click **Fork**.
3. Fork it to your GitHub account or organization.
4. Rename the fork if you want.
5. Replace the examples with your real profile, services, projects, research or pilot information.
6. Add evidence only when it is appropriate and verifiable.
7. Connect the resulting repository to MyZubster, Zorgax, the Marketplace or a pilot when useful.

Your fork does not have to stay identical to the template. It becomes your own working repository.

## Fork vs. contribution to the original

Use a **fork** when you want to create your own identity, project or community repository.

Use a **pull request** to the original template only when you want to improve the reusable template for everyone.

## Privacy and evidence

Keep private addresses, credentials, identity documents, sensitive personal information and confidential partner material out of public repositories. Clearly distinguish demos, proposals and self-declared information from measured, verified or independently validated results.
