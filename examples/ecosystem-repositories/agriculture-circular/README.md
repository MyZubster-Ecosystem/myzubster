# Agriculture & Circular Farming Example

Example profile for farmers, growers, cooperatives and circular-agriculture projects.

## What we offer
- cultivation knowledge
- local food and material production
- circular resource use
- field sites for pilots and research
- environmental observations and evidence

## What we need
- researchers and students
- developers for monitoring and traceability tools
- water, soil and biodiversity expertise
- logistics and local-market connections

## Marketplace examples
- local agricultural services
- workshops and training
- plant or soil monitoring support
- circular products and materials
- pilot participation

## Zorgax
Zorgax can help organize project evidence, participant profiles, needs, milestones and links to LIFE-oriented pilots.

## Evidence and privacy
Publish only verifiable observations and public project outputs. Withhold exact private farm/garden locations unless explicitly approved.
