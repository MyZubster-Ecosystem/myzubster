# Example Repository — University Department / Research Lab

## Who we are
A demonstration profile for university departments, laboratories, research groups and student teams joining the MyZubster ecosystem.

## What we can offer
- applied research
- student projects and theses
- open-source contributions
- pilot design and evaluation
- data analysis and MRV methodologies
- interdisciplinary collaboration with communities, SMEs and public bodies

## What we need from the ecosystem
- real-world pilot cases
- community partners and field evidence
- developer support for prototypes
- public datasets where appropriate
- clear research questions and impact objectives

## Marketplace / collaboration examples
- research collaboration calls
- student project briefs
- prototype validation
- data-analysis services
- workshops and training
- open-source research tooling

## Zorgax role
Zorgax can help convert community needs into research questions, organize project documentation, support onboarding and map possible collaborators and pilots.

## Public evidence
Use DOI/publications, public repositories, project pages and reproducible outputs. Do not publish confidential research data, unpublished participant data, ethics-sensitive information or personal student records.
