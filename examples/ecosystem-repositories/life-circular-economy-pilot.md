# Example Repository — LIFE / Circular Economy Pilot

## Who we are
A demonstration profile for an individual, organization or community entering MyZubster as a LIFE-oriented circular economy pilot.

## Possible pilot areas
- circular water
- reusable or traceable products
- environmental evidence / MRV
- local repair and reuse systems
- fermentation and food circularity
- community logistics
- culture-led local regeneration

## What we can offer
- a real-world use case
- measurable field observations
- pilot participants and local knowledge
- evidence that can be documented in GitHub
- collaboration with universities, developers and communities

## What we need
- technical integration support
- research partners
- measurement methodology
- privacy-safe data structures
- marketplace or service integration
- Zorgax-supported onboarding and reporting

## Suggested repository structure
- `README.md` — pilot overview
- `evidence/` — public, privacy-safe evidence
- `data/` — sanitized machine-readable observations
- `docs/` — methodology and stakeholder notes
- `issues/` — open tasks and validation needs

## Zorgax role
Zorgax can help participants define the pilot, identify missing evidence, draft tasks, connect collaborators and prepare public summaries.

## Evidence rules
A roadmap, issue, PR or merge is not itself proof of deployment, partnership, adoption, funding or payment. Claims should be backed by verifiable evidence.
