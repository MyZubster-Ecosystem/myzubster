# Example Repository — Local Services

## Who we are
A demonstration profile for local service providers entering the MyZubster ecosystem: household help, neighbourhood services, small businesses and independent workers.

## What we can offer
- practical local services
- short-notice help
- maintenance and household support
- community referrals
- local knowledge and logistics

## What we need from the ecosystem
- digital onboarding and profile setup
- help publishing clear service listings
- scheduling and communication tools
- local visibility
- support from developers and community moderators

## Marketplace examples
- home assistance
- delivery or transport help
- cleaning and maintenance
- neighbourhood errands
- small local services

## Zorgax role
Zorgax can help structure service descriptions, collect requirements, prepare listings and connect users with the right local providers.

## Public evidence
Use public service descriptions, certifications where appropriate, references the provider chooses to publish and completed-project evidence. Never publish private addresses, identity documents or customer personal data.
