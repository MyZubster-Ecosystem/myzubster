# Animals, Pet Services & Rescue Example

Example profile for shelters, pet sitters, volunteers and animal-support communities.

## What we offer
- pet sitting and local care
- adoption support
- lost/found animal coordination
- shelter volunteering
- responsible pet services and supplies

## What we need
- local volunteers
- developers for alerts and matching tools
- designers and communicators
- university partners for welfare research

## Marketplace examples
- dogsitting and petsitting
- pet transport support
- accessories and supplies
- volunteer coordination
- adoption-related services

## Privacy and welfare
Do not publish private home addresses or sensitive owner data. Animal listings should avoid unverifiable ownership or medical claims.
