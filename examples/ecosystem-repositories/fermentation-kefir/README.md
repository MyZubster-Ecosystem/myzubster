# Fermentation & Kefir Community Example

Example profile for fermentation groups, kefir communities, food labs and wellness-oriented makers joining MyZubster.

## What we offer
- fermentation knowledge and peer education
- workshops and community exchange
- food-culture documentation
- collaboration with researchers and circular food projects

## What we need
- university and laboratory collaboration
- safe documentation and quality practices
- developers for community tools and traceability
- local partners for workshops and pilots

## Marketplace examples
- workshops
- educational materials
- community events
- non-medical wellness education
- pilot participation

## Safety
Do not present community practices as medical treatment. Keep health claims evidence-based and avoid publishing private participant information.
