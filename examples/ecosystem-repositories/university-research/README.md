# University & Research Example

Example profile for a university group, student team or research lab joining MyZubster.

## What we offer
- courses, workshops and mentoring
- research collaboration
- datasets and reproducible experiments
- GitHub projects and open-source contributions
- student theses and pilot projects

## What we need
- real-world communities for field testing
- developers for prototypes
- circular-economy and LIFE pilot partners
- artists and communicators for dissemination

## Marketplace examples
- research support
- tutoring and training
- data analysis
- prototype development
- scientific communication

## Zorgax
Zorgax can help onboard participants, structure project profiles, connect skills and needs, and document pilot evidence.

## Evidence
Link only public, verifiable outputs such as repositories, publications, datasets, demos and issue/PR contributions.

## Privacy
Do not publish student personal data, private addresses, unpublished research, credentials or confidential partner information.
