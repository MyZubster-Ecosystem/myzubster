## 🤝 Partnership Guidelines

### Partnership Tiers

| Tier | Contribution | Benefits |
|------|--------------|----------|
| **Platinum** | $50k+/year | Logo placement, advisory board, quarterly reports |
| **Gold** | $10k-$49k/year | Logo placement, monthly reports |
| **Silver** | $1k-$9k/year | Logo placement, quarterly reports |
| **Bronze** | <$1k/year | Name listing, annual report |

### Partnership Process

1. **Outreach**
   - Identify potential partners
   - Research their values and goals
   - Prepare tailored proposal

2. **Negotiation**
   - Discuss partnership terms
   - Align on expectations
   - Formalize agreement

3. **Onboarding**
   - Welcome package
   - Logo and branding guidelines
   - Communication channels

4. **Ongoing Management**
   - Regular check-ins
   - Impact reporting
   - Renewal discussions
